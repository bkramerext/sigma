# T279 — Invite me from the sign-in pill

**Issue:** [#279](https://github.com/bkramerext/sigma/issues/279)  
**Owner:** Blue  
**Base:** current `main`

## Objective

Let a guest find the invitation request through the existing SIGMA account
pills. Reuse the T81 verified invitation-request backend and T88 Google sign-in
flow. A request records interest for administrator review; it never grants
membership.

## Behavior and acceptance

- Guests can open an **Invite me** action from the existing pill row. Its dialog
  explains that Google sign-in is needed to verify the account and links to the
  established invitation-intent login path.
- After Google verifies an uninvited account, the existing invitation dialog
  uses the server-issued denied-access identity and submits to the existing
  invitation endpoint. It does not accept browser-supplied identity.
- Submission is single-flight, shows recoverable errors with safe retry, and on
  success displays the established confirmation and removes the Invite me pill.
- Authorized members and administrators are not offered Invite me. Existing
  Google sign-in, membership enforcement, guest Samples access, and sponsored
  model controls remain intact.
- Invitation and privacy behavior remains visible at the collection point;
  existing duplicate protection, persistence, notifications, and administrator
  review remain in force.

## Verification

Deterministic cloud-auth tests, a real-Chrome desktop/mobile acceptance check,
frontend typecheck/build, and `./sigma test`. No live OAuth, email, provider,
paid, or production operations.
