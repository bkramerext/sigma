## Objective

Continue the existing invitation workflow on the hosted Access Not Granted
screen. This workflow does not belong in About. Replace its `mailto:` action
with a verified server submission, durable administrator-review record, and
authenticated email notification.

## Acceptance

- Request Invitation submits to the SIGMA backend; it never opens an email app.
- Identity comes only from the server-issued, short-lived cookie created after
  Google verified the denied sign-in. Browser-supplied identity is ignored.
- Requests persist with identity, timestamp, notification/review status, and are
  available only to administrators.
- Notifications go to `zetacontinuation@gmail.com` through authenticated TLS
  SMTP. The authenticated mailbox is the sender; secrets are supplied through
  protected runtime secret files.
- Duplicate requests are idempotent, invalid/unverified requests fail, and
  repeat submissions are rate limited.
- Success replaces the button with exactly: “Glad you're interested! Your
  request has been sent along to the right person!”
- Failure retains the button and shows an error.

## Constraints

- Preserve the existing access-denied location and surrounding hosted flow.
- Do not add invitation UI to About or trust visitor-provided email addresses.
- Do not spoof the sender, embed credentials, or fall back to `mailto:`.
- Record the runtime SMTP credential requirement and unverified live delivery
  clearly; no credential or live email may be fabricated.
