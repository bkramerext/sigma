# T35: Alpha release readiness and sign-off

**Status:** Required for final alpha release; unassigned. **Priority:** Release gate, without interrupting active Codex coordination and integration.

## Objective

Establish and execute one evidence-based checklist for shipping SIGMA's final alpha. Clearly distinguish engineering readiness from unverified live-model research quality.

## Checklist scope

- Reconcile required release tickets, including T31 README, T32 WOLF, T33 post-alpha roadmap, T34 sample polish, and T36 clean installation acceptance, alongside product/integration/cloud requirements.
- Verify ticket and PR state against current `main`; identify outstanding blockers and explicit deferrals.
- Audit user-facing and developer documentation for obsolete instructions, inconsistent feature claims, broken links, and misleading descriptions of provider costs, sandbox security, and scientific verification.
- Confirm release version, changelog/release notes, Git tag, and reproducible source revision.
- Summarize test evidence, browser acceptance, deployment status, supported environments, and known limitations.
- Confirm privacy/security and credential-handling disclosures, billing-mode behavior, and recovery/failure expectations appropriate to an alpha.
- Prepare a concise release decision record with blockers, accepted limitations, and the human's explicit go/no-go sign-off.

## Acceptance

1. One durable checklist records PASS, BLOCKED, UNVERIFIED, or explicitly accepted limitation for each release gate, with evidence links.
2. No alpha requirement is silently reclassified as post-alpha work.
3. Release notes and documentation accurately describe shipped behavior; no implied independent mathematical discovery or proof.
4. Version and tag identify the exact released revision after final integration.
5. The human explicitly approves the alpha release; no autonomous publication or release tagging without authorization.

## Scheduling

This is required for final alpha release. Draft/review it during a genuine coordination gap; worker blockers, PR review, and integration take precedence. Do not dispatch without human agreement.

Any worker-facing assignment must state: **Do not coordinate or communicate directly with other Codex workers. Report dependencies, conflicts, and requests for other workers to your ChatGPT coordinator through the human.**
