# T45 — High priority: Long-form TeX paper generation under model output limits

**Priority:** HIGH (paper quality / core research output)
**Status:** Implemented on `t45-long-paper-generation`; pending coordinator review.
Implementation was explicitly authorized by the user after the original investigation-only proposal.
**Suggested owner:** Green (T20 paper-generation familiarity)
**Related:** T20 AI-authored papers, report/export pipeline, provider output and context budgets

## Problem

SIGMA can generate AI-authored LaTeX papers, but a single bounded model response cannot reliably hold a substantive research paper. Character caps may truncate prose or TeX mid-command, and provider output-token, request-envelope, context-window, and stored-field limits are not interchangeable. Simply raising one character limit is unlikely to support long papers robustly.

We need a reliable path from a potentially large, evidence-rich research record to a coherent, complete, well-typeset paper without losing source attribution or pretending omitted material was reviewed.

## Investigation

1. **Map actual limits and failure modes.** Trace T20's end-to-end path from evidence snapshot and prompt assembly through provider call, structured response, persistence, validation, TeX rendering, PDF compilation, and UI/download. Identify every relevant byte/character/token/context/envelope/field limit, with file/line references and tests. Separate intentional safety bounds from accidental reuse of limits intended for other features. Determine what happens today on overflow, partial output, timeout, cancellation, and restart.
2. **Evaluate architectures, not just larger caps.** Compare one-shot generation with a staged process: evidence inventory and outline; section-by-section generation using explicit source references; durable section checkpoints; cross-section consistency/claim review; deterministic TeX assembly and PDF compilation. Consider bounded retries of individual sections, model context constraints, token budgets, cost and latency, and versioning.
3. **Preserve evidence and coherence.** Define how the full frozen research snapshot is indexed, how evidence is selected for each section, how important contradictory or negative results are retained, and how cross-references, definitions, theorem numbering, citations, notation, and conclusions stay consistent. The model must not invent proof steps, external citations, or verification.
4. **Define operational behavior.** Show honest progress across stages, support cancellation/restart/resume without duplicating completed work, prevent accidental extra provider calls, preserve immutable source and paper versions, and surface actionable failures. Explicitly account for ChatGPT-plan vs API billing identity and limits; no silent fallback.
5. **Measure representative cases.** Use an existing small paper and a deliberately larger evidence fixture whose desired paper exceeds today's single-response budget. Deterministic/mock tests first. Compare output completeness, evidence fidelity, TeX/PDF compilation, time and projected usage. Do not make live/paid provider calls without explicit authorization.

## Implementation contract

The one-shot workflow remains available for complete snapshots up to 100,000
bytes. Larger snapshots use bounded long-form authoring. Both workflows bind
paper generation to a complete normalized, immutable source snapshot and its
SHA-256. The full snapshot is attached to the saved paper; over-bound snapshots
fail before provider use rather than silently dropping data.

Long-form workflow:

1. Flatten project, strategy, session, Outcome, artifact, agentic-run, and step
   records into UTF-8-safe fragments with stable source IDs and field paths.
2. Index bounded batches, recording a covered or omitted status for every
   field. Keep negative/limitation notes and source-linked conflict/gap notes
   through checkpointing and reduction.
3. Author a shared claim register and bounded outline, then draft each section
   independently from its assigned source-linked notes.
4. Validate claim and source references against the complete snapshot, append
   deterministic saved research records, compile TeX, and publish one
   immutable version only after successful compilation.

Preflight reports snapshot size, evidence batches, calls by stage, API-key
requested-output ceilings, compatible checkpoint progress, and prior/current
provider-call attempts. Generation requires explicit confirmation and resumes
only against the same snapshot, project, provider identity, model, and effort.
Completed work is checkpointed privately and reused. Restart requires explicit
confirmation and retains previous-attempt accounting. A provider request that
finishes immediately before a process crash but before checkpoint persistence
may be repeated on retry.

## Research integrity and Outcome snapshots

Follow the shared research-integrity contract in issue #71. A paper snapshot
retains each complete exported session record and its Outcome object, plus the
session's full artifacts as separately referenced sources. It does not limit
the snapshot schema to today's Outcome keys: if an exported Outcome includes
optional `outcome.conclusion` Markdown, that field is copied and archived as
part of the session record. The paper does not synthesize or backfill a missing
conclusion. It preserves the available conclusion alongside the full evidence.

Long-form TeX adds a source-linked appendix containing saved structured
reasoning steps and epistemic labels, artifact references, Skeptic assessments
and objections, methods, limitations/caveats, negative findings, contradictions,
and unresolved obligations. It also includes preserved negative/limitation
notes and source-linked conflict/gap notes from indexing. These are saved
records, not independent verification or unrestricted model chain-of-thought.

Every extractor omission keeps its source ID, field pointer, and reason in the
paper record. Research-field omissions are treated as material except for
known bookkeeping fields (IDs, timestamps, sequence/cycle values, and record
kind). Material omissions trigger a prominent TeX limitation disclosure and
are visible in the paper preview and author-review warning. The abstract keeps
its substantive synthesis after a prominent disclosure, labels it provisional,
and limits it to covered evidence. Each conclusion is preserved and qualified
as provisional, with an explicit statement that omitted material was not
reviewed. The paper draws no conclusion from omitted material or its absence.
All `established` claims become `unresolved`, so missing evidence cannot
silently support an established mathematical claim. The complete frozen
snapshot and omission ledger remain available for review.

The snapshot and paper consumer retain optional exported `outcome.conclusion`
when present and leave it absent when the saved Outcome has no conclusion. The
paper never backfills an absent conclusion. Deterministic regression fixtures
verify that reasoning, Skeptic assessments, limitations, optional conclusion,
and source references survive snapshot creation and TeX assembly.

## Current bounds

| Stage | Bound | Behavior at the bound |
|---|---:|---|
| Frozen evidence snapshot | 20 MB | Reject before provider use; no evidence is dropped |
| Evidence request/index | 48 KB; 100 fields; 3 extracted notes; 512 batches | Record every field as covered or omitted; retain source-linked conflicts and omissions |
| Long-paper outline | 220 KB input; 48 sections; 200 claims | Reject malformed or over-bound structured output |
| Provider requests | 120 seconds each; 593 calls maximum | Stop without publishing; checkpoints remain resumable |
| API-key response | 12,000 requested output tokens; 1 MB response | Reject incomplete or oversized response |
| ChatGPT-plan response | 4 MB streamed response | Reject incomplete or oversized response |
| Authored section | 250 KB serialized output | Reject oversized section; prior sections remain checkpointed |
| TeX compilation for papers | 50 MB source; 300 seconds; 80 MB PDF | Compile in Tectonic's untrusted mode; publish no PDF on failure |
| Saved paper version | 100 MB including snapshot and PDF | Reject publication atomically |

These are finite safety and usage bounds, not unlimited-generation promises.
The deterministic report exporter retains its separate prior limits.
