# T38 — Operation Narrow Passage: adversarial research-quality evaluation

Status: **OPEN; unassigned**. Evaluation ticket, not a feature or release signoff.

## Purpose

Exercise the existing `samples/operation-narrow-passage` through SIGMA's normal research workflow and assess whether the resulting research is useful, skeptical, adaptive, and honest about uncertainty. A polished report or successful session termination is not sufficient evidence of quality.

This is a **fictional geopolitical crisis scenario**. Evaluate the quality of decision support and reasoning, not operational effectiveness of military action. Do not invent intelligence, verify fictional events as real, or turn this into a tactical weapons-planning exercise.

## Evaluation design

1. **Inspect the shipped sample and workflow.** Record its exact problem, existing strategies, assumptions, model configuration, and any pre-existing evidence. Preserve the canonical sample; create a working copy for evaluation.
2. **Define an independent rubric before seeing model outputs.** Assess (a) distinguishing facts, claims, and unknowns; (b) identifying decision-relevant assumptions; (c) comparing feasible high-level policy/diplomatic/humanitarian response options and tradeoffs; (d) seeking discriminating evidence; (e) skepticism about intelligence authenticity, escalation, treaty/legal interpretations, and second-order effects; (f) updating recommendations when assumptions change; (g) actionable communication of uncertainty, reversibility, and decision triggers; and (h) provenance and correct outcome labeling.
3. **Run deterministic/offline adversarial cases** through the actual evaluation interfaces where feasible. Include contradictory or unreliable incident videos, urgent humanitarian needs, a false high-confidence intelligence claim, changed timelines, and a later correction to a previous assumption. Test whether an attractive narrative survives scrutiny without evidence.
4. **Evaluate real agent behavior separately**, using a bounded normal-workflow provider run only after explicit human authorization for any paid calls. Record model/effort, budgets, inputs, ordered artifact IDs, objections, strategy decisions, outcomes, costs, and errors. Do not treat mocked responses as independently produced analysis.
5. **Independent substantive review.** For each major recommendation, identify evidence, assumptions, strongest objection, alternative, and what new information would change the recommendation. Identify contradictions, generic or repeated skepticism, invented facts, and unsupported certainty. Judge whether the output actually improves a human decision-maker's understanding.
6. **Assess continuity.** Test whether a later session meaningfully incorporates previous objections and corrections, rather than repeating them or silently dropping them.
7. **Localize findings** as model reasoning, skeptic behavior, strategy choice, orchestration, memory, labeling, report, or UI. Propose general fixes rather than scenario-specific prompt hacks.

## Acceptance

- A durable evaluation report links to exact session/artifact evidence and includes the preregistered rubric, scored/qualified findings, failures, and counterexamples.
- Clearly separates **MET / UNMET / UNVERIFIED** for each evaluation criterion, and distinguishes code inspection, deterministic test results, and independently observed live runs.
- Includes at least one adversarial case where the correct response is to withhold judgment or change a recommendation, and verifies whether SIGMA does so.
- Records whether skepticism is substantive and decision-relevant, not merely boilerplate.
- Provides a prioritized, bounded improvement backlog with regression criteria; no claims of general competence from one sample.
- Does not alter the existing sample's historical evidence or silently fabricate research artifacts.
- No paid provider calls without explicit human approval. No broad UI redesign, military tactical optimization, or deployment/security waiver.
