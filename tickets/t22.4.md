## Decision and trigger

The human approved omitting backup infrastructure and restore exercises from the external-evaluation alpha to speed delivery, **not canceling them**. ## Implementation requirements

- Design consistent recovery points across Google auth/access records, workspace identity mapping, private project trees, session/events/artifacts, research job ledger, provider-attempt and budget reservations, and configuration needed to restore them safely.
- Automate encrypted off-host backups with controlled service identity, storage permissions, retention, and documented cadence. Prevent secrets leakage, tenant mixing and public Demo exposure.
- Ensure backups cannot silently omit new schema fields or active job/call accounting. Define safe handling of in-flight and ambiguous provider requests on restore; never automatically replay an uncertain charged call.
- Document disaster-recovery runbook: fresh host provisioning, retrieval, decryption, migrations, restore, security validation, credential/session rotation decision, and rollback. Include recovery-point/time objectives appropriate to the adopted hosting provider and budget.
- Add reproducible backup and clean-host restore validation, including two isolated users and a denied/revoked user, private artifacts, admin/auth mapping, durable jobs, budget ceilings, unknown charges and cross-user API restrictions.
- Verify repeated backups, failed/incomplete backups, corrupt archives, unsafe permissions, and restoration from a previous version. Alerts/health must detect backup failure.

## Acceptance

A coordinator-reviewed PR and operational runbook demonstrate a complete backup and clean-host restore against the real deployment architecture (not just copied test files). A restored deployment preserves tenant isolation and accounts conservatively for model charges. Evidence records commands, expected/observed recovery, redacted configuration, and MET/UNMET/UNVERIFIED requirements. Human authorizes post-alpha release only after this ticket is MET or expressly revises the policy.
