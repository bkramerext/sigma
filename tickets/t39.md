# T39 — Riemann Hypothesis sample: adversarial research-quality evaluation

Status: **OPEN; unassigned**. Independent quality evaluation, not a claim to solve RH.

## Objective

Exercise the existing `samples/riemann-hypothesis-research` through SIGMA's normal workflows and independently assess whether its agents behave like rigorous mathematical researchers: choosing bounded useful investigations, identifying proof obligations, exposing invalid inferences, learning from failures, and reporting defensible partial results. The project-level goal is RH; individual strategies are **narrow investigations**, not requirements to solve RH.

Do not overwrite the shipped sample's historical evidence. Use a fresh working copy and preserve reproducible evaluation records.

## Evaluation protocol

1. **Baseline and precommitment.** Inspect sample, strategy definitions, agent contracts, session/evidence/report pipeline, existing tests and known limitations. Before model outputs, write a falsifiable rubric and a set of expected traps, with independently checkable mathematical facts and failure criteria. No answer leakage in model prompts.
2. **Skepticism under pressure.** Test plausible but invalid RH-adjacent reasoning: extrapolating finite zero checks to all zeros; assuming spectral analogy establishes a self-adjoint operator or bidirectional zero correspondence; confusing a zero-free region near Re(s)=1 with the full critical strip; omitting analytic continuation/domain/multiplicity assumptions; circularly invoking an RH-equivalent estimate; and treating numerically suggested cancellation as proof. Check whether the Skeptic names the precise broken inference and an informative next test.
3. **Strategic judgment.** Evaluate whether the agents choose a bounded lemma or discriminating counterexample test, prioritize high-information obligations, revise or abandon stalled paths, and avoid generic research-program prose. Compare at least two viable next-step options using likely information gain and cost.
4. **Continuity and provenance.** Test multiple sessions and strategies: preserve unresolved objections and counterexamples, avoid recycling refuted claims, distinguish inherited evidence from fresh derivation, and correctly credit strategy contributions. Coordinate overlap with T37 research-memory changes; test current main and recheck after integration if relevant.
5. **Epistemic correctness.** Distinguish established theorem, derivation, conditional claim, heuristic, computation, and speculation. Independently verify any claimed exact lemma; do not accept a fluent narrative or model-to-model agreement as proof. Correctly mark unresolved outcomes; never assert RH was proved based solely on agent output.
6. **Product presentation.** Check that saved artifacts, session status, strategy assessments, reports, and UI convey what was actually established, what failed, and what should be investigated next. Preserve useful negative findings.
7. **Execution evidence.** Run deterministic offline adversarial scenarios first. Separately run a bounded real-provider research session through normal SIGMA flow **only with explicit human approval for paid calls**. Record model/effort, configured limits, prompts/contexts, ordered artifact IDs, judgments, outcomes, usage, errors, and whether verification was deterministic, code-inspected, or independently observed.

## Acceptance and handoff

- Durable independent evaluation report with precommitted rubric, evidence references, actual outcomes, strengths, defects, and prioritized generalizable improvements.
- Per-substantive-requirement **MET / UNMET / UNVERIFIED**, explicitly distinguishing mocked tests from real-model behavior and independent mathematical checking.
- At least one adversarial false-proof case and one bounded partial-result case, with explicit evaluation of whether SIGMA appropriately rejects, qualifies, or credits them.
- At least one multi-session continuity check; note dependencies and any coverage overlap with T37.
- Any proposed fix targets a general research-quality failure, not hard-coded RH responses. Corrections require separate scoped implementation/review if outside this ticket.
- No paid provider calls without explicit human authorization; no new sample; no broad UX redesign; no release-signoff claims.

Follow `docs/SOFTWARE_FACTORY.md`; worker coordination goes through the human.

## Bug discovery, correction, and regression gate

This exercise is also an **active product bug hunt**, not merely an evaluation report. Exercise normal user workflows end to end and look for functional, state/persistence, concurrency/restart, provider/network, evidence/provenance, outcome-labeling, report/export, and UI bugs. For every reproducible defect:

1. Record exact reproduction steps, expected versus actual behavior, relevant session/artifact IDs, logs or screenshots where appropriate, and severity.
2. Determine whether the issue is a software defect, weak model reasoning, or an evaluation-fixture limitation; do not confuse them.
3. **Get the bug fixed**, not just documented: implement a bounded correction within authorized ticket scope, or create a linked, prioritized fix ticket and assign it through the coordinator when ownership or scope differs. Do not silently defer a material bug.
4. Add a regression test at the appropriate layer, rerun relevant automated and manual checks, and verify the actual corrected behavior.
5. Track every discovered bug through **fixed and verified**, or explicitly **open with owner, priority, and reason**. Mandatory or severe unresolved defects block declaring this evaluation complete without informed human acceptance.

Apply `docs/SOFTWARE_FACTORY.md` review gates, including independent code/test review and per-requirement MET/UNMET/UNVERIFIED. Never use a green suite or attractive sample report as a substitute for investigating observed defects.
