# T33: Post-alpha feature roadmap from ideas and brainstorms

**Status:** Alpha release planning requirement; discussion pending. **Owner:** Unassigned. **Priority:** Complete roadmap before final alpha release, without diverting active Codex coordination or release integration.

## Objective

Review the ideas, brainstorms, deferred proposals, and known gaps accumulated during SIGMA development. Distill them into a **forward-looking, prioritized feature list for work after the alpha release**. This ticket produces a roadmap, not the features themselves.

## Sources to review

- Brainstorm and ideas notes, including `WOLF_IDEAS.md` where relevant.
- `docs/DECISIONS.md`, `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/PROJECT.md`, and `docs/SOFTWARE_FACTORY.md`.
- Ticket history, including deferred, rejected, incomplete, and follow-up items.
- PR descriptions, `messages/` handoffs, acceptance findings, and documented limitations.
- Relevant SIGMA planning conversations and user decisions, where available. Do not infer decisions from incomplete history.

## Deliverable

A durable **post-alpha feature roadmap** that:
- Groups candidates by coherent themes, such as research intelligence, verification, computation, UX, collaboration, deployment, and reliability.
- States each candidate's user value, rough scope, dependencies, and rationale.
- Distinguishes committed follow-ups, promising proposals, speculative research, and ideas explicitly rejected or deferred by the human.
- Prioritizes a practical near-term sequence while preserving longer-term possibilities.
- Identifies evidence or experiments needed before committing to uncertain features.
- Links candidates to original notes, tickets, or discussions wherever possible.
- Clearly separates **alpha release blockers** from **post-alpha enhancements**; do not silently defer an existing alpha requirement.

## Acceptance

1. Review the available idea and decision record systematically rather than relying on recent memory alone.
2. Deduplicate overlapping suggestions and resolve contradictions with later decisions.
3. Present a concise prioritized roadmap with traceable sources and clear uncertainty.
4. Discuss the shortlist and priorities with the human before treating any feature as approved.
5. Record the roadmap in the repository for future planning. No post-alpha implementation is authorized by this ticket.

## Scheduling

**Roadmap document is required for the final alpha release; roadmap features are not.** Discuss and prepare it during a genuine coordination gap. Keeping workers productive, resolving blockers, reviewing/merging PRs, and completing alpha integration take precedence. Do not dispatch a worker or launch feature implementation without human agreement.

Any later worker assignment must explicitly prohibit direct coordination or communication with other Codex workers; dependencies route through the human and ChatGPT coordinator.
