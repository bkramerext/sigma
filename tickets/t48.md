# T48: Distinguish differing strategy findings from genuine contradictions

**Priority:** Medium-high  
**Owner:** Gray (queue after T47 is merged)  
**Status:** Pending, not assigned  
**Related:** T47 / PR #72, Red's structured outcomes PR #70, issue #71

## Problem
T47 currently detects a cross-strategy conflict when two achieved claims differ after case/whitespace normalization. Distinct wording is not evidence of contradiction. Two strategies may provide equivalent formulations, complementary bounds and constructions, or genuinely incompatible claims.

## Scope
- Replace string-inequality conflict inference with conservative, evidence-grounded classification.
- Without explicit supported contradiction evidence, describe differing claims as **distinct candidate findings** or **relationship undetermined**, not conflicting.
- Preserve per-strategy attribution, source artifact references, review status, unresolved objections, and unverified labels.
- Report an actual contradiction only when a saved, inspectable assessment identifies incompatible propositions and cites the relevant claims/evidence. Do not infer mathematical equivalence or inconsistency using string comparison.
- Keep deterministic Conclusion generation and no new provider calls. Avoid scenario-specific heuristics.
- Reconcile with Red's structured Outcome representation if merged, without discarding older saved sessions.

## Acceptance
1. Equivalent wording (e.g., two formulations of the same formula) is not called a contradiction.
2. Complementary findings (upper bound and attaining construction) are not called a contradiction.
3. Two unsupported, different candidate answers are presented distinctly without declaring a verified contradiction.
4. An explicit evidence-linked contradictory assessment can be reported as a **reviewed, unverified conflict** with both sides and provenance.
5. Legacy sessions remain readable; reports and TeX source snapshots preserve the Conclusion and evidence.
6. Unit tests cover these cases and real Chrome verifies readable desktop/mobile presentation; `./sigma test` passes. No live/paid model calls.
