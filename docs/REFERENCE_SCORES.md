The most successful examples are Stanford's Computer Science writing guide, Reed's Economics research guide, and Arizona's Philosophy argument-analysis handout: concise, practical, and primarily text.

The weakest remaining choices are the NASEM Physics summary and the NASA Engineering page, mainly because they retain website navigation or broader explanatory material. I kept them for their methodological substance.
