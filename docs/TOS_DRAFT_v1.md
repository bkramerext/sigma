## Proposed wording

### Experimental research software

SIGMA is an experimental research workbench. Features and results may be
incomplete, inaccurate, or unavailable. Research outputs, including generated
text, calculations, code, and reports, are not guaranteed to be correct or
complete.

### Your review and use

Review research outputs and verify important claims independently before
relying on them or sharing them. A successful computation or agent review does
not by itself establish a proof or a correct result.

Use SIGMA only with material you are authorized to provide. When you choose an
AI feature, relevant prompts and project context may be sent to the provider
for the selected connection. Review the applicable provider terms and settings
before using that feature.
