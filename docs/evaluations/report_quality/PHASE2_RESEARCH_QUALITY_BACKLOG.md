# Phase 2 backlog — improve the underlying research, **after** paper-writing quality converges

**User directive, 2026-11-01:** SIGMA's current experiment is **Phase 1: improve complete academic-paper synthesis from the same already-saved, frozen research**. Phase 2 is an independent, later effort to strengthen the research itself. Do not silently rerun or enrich the underlying investigations during Phase 1. Continue Phase 1 iterations against the three frozen snapshots until there is evidence of convergence in whole-paper quality, not until a single bug or PDF is fixed.

## Phase-1 convergence criteria (must actually be measured)

1. AI Agent: outcome observability, evaluator calibration, retry delivery versus effect commitment, finite-roster inference and retracted false generalizations accurately explained, with no fictitious measurements.
2. Sovereignty: recover coherent four-comparator / policy / bounded-liability / break-even / sensitive-stratum / pilot argument and explicitly flag incomplete reviews and source limitations, surviving fallback-planner failures.
3. Xenopus: clear theoretical identifiability, equal-exposure contrast with restricted assumptions, latency/redox/spatial competing explanations, falsifiable *proposed* experiments and no invented biological data.
4. Cross-paper: accurate claim-specific citation/source mapping, independent appendix controls, complete reviewed articles, safe proofreading, genuine TeX/PDF usability, and minimal unsupported/general filler.
5. Re-run at least one substantial later generation with frozen snapshots and compare to each project's **best prior baseline**, including critical negative findings. A single nondeterministic favorable draft is not a replication; document residual instability.
6. Stop Phase 1 only on a defensible plateau of substantive quality and absence of newly discovered, high-impact regressions. Record unresolved issues without pretending publication acceptance.

## Candidate Phase-2 research-quality investments (not implementation authorization today)

- **AI Agent Reliability:** independently executed fault-injection experiments across retry/drop/delay/commit/observer paths; valid outcome oracles; randomized task clusters and reference calibration; proofs of soundness assumptions; measured coverage and failure rates; explicit population transport questions.
- **AI Sovereignty:** retrieve/verify primary MindRouter documentation and PEARC paper; validate real current institutional policies/contractual bounds and provider pricing; run implementation/model-capacity audit; independently check the N=1800 sufficient-sample certificate and its assumptions; complete failed last Skeptic review; measure approved four-baseline pilots and subgroup utility when ethical and feasible.
- **Xenopus Tadpoles:** inspect real GEO accession matrices/manifests (species, stage, pairing, batch, biological unit); systematic literature novelty assessment; test whether temporal receptivity mechanism is coherent with known RIC/ECM work; design and execute authorized controlled ERK-pulse, latency, spatial and redox studies with proper replicates and independent biological validation.
- **Across research agents:** improve retrieval of primary sources, transparent provenance, independent evidence arbitration, contradiction handling, negative-results preservation, research-stop/continue criterion, and reproducible experiment declarations. Carefully distinguish model debate from an external referee.

## Handoff data to preserve during Phase 1

For each issue, record exact evidence/source IDs, manuscript location, whether the problem is **missing research** or **incorrect/insufficient synthesis of existing research**, and an actionable later method of resolution. A paper may not invent results to close these gaps. The final Phase-1 review should point here for research-dependency issues that cannot be fixed merely by editing the writer.
