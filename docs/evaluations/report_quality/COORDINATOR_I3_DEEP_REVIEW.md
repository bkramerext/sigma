## Research-writing standards that shape this review

- The ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Standards are method-specific. A technical proposal's conceptual merits do not imply that an engineering artifact has undergone a completed empirical evaluation. Methodological/analytical work, however, can make a legitimate bounded contribution without inventing a benchmark. Source: https://www2.sigsoft.org/EmpiricalStandards/docs/standards
- "Ten simple rules for structuring papers" (PLOS Computational Biology, 2017) emphasizes one central idea, context-content-conclusion flow, a credible logical progression, results that justify the conclusion, and discussion that anticipates criticism. These principles inform argument architecture rather than a universal IMRAD checkbox. Source: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5619685/
- For executed animal studies ARRIVE 2.0 requires transparent design, biological units, interventions, controls and results. A hypothesis paper that has run **no** animal experiment must describe these as proposed experimental obligations and must not fabricate completed experimental sections. Source: https://arriveguidelines.org/arrive-guidelines
- ICMJE's manuscript guidance and discipline-specific reporting guidelines aid transparency and clarity. A source-linked article must say what the method actually did, what the result supports, what remains missing and why the central inference is warranted. A checklist or a correctly formed PDF does not certify scientific truth.

**Practical editorial contract:** A paper should have an answerable central question; a precise thesis; propositions with individually inspectable assumptions, evidence and verification stage; a coherent sequence of claims and counterclaims; a fair evaluation of alternatives; limitations attached to the claims they qualify; a discussion that interprets rather than restates the register; and an abstract and conclusion with the same epistemic scope. Each paragraph should advance a distinct part of that argument. Do not inflate words/pages, enumerate evidence instead of reasoning, or substitute a generic template for actual findings.

## Project A — AI Agent Reliability: deepen a substantively improved analytical manuscript

**Best text baseline: I2.** I1's metadata-as-headline-claims and generic 20-word conclusion failed the central research argument. I2 now explains how fixed-protocol outcomes differ from report acceptance, why reference validity matters, the indistinguishable precommit/commit-but-ack-lost histories, and why retry issuance is not the same as duplicate external commitment. It correctly differentiates effect-first duplication from marker-first suppression and uses a conditional, non-empirical conclusion. These are genuine gains to preserve.

**Independent mathematical audit:** With Y=actual correctness, O=automated acceptance, q=P(O=1), f=P(O=1,Y=0), g=P(O=0,Y=1), the event partition gives p=P(Y=1)=q-f+g. An apparent q is not p until error events are constrained independently. Under the *restricted* model of one retry that definitely commits, choosing retry with probability r across two observationally identical histories yields worst-case failure max(r,1-r) >= 1/2. It is invalid to apply the bound when retries may be lost; the paper preserves that counterexample. The difference between independent task clusters and multiple correlated runs within a fixed task is material to statistical interval validity. These are conditional analytic statements, not population measurements.

**Frontier improvement:** Show a rigorous inferential chain: (1) declare task distribution, allowed tools, execution policy, time horizon and success indicator; (2) separate true outcome from grader and reference observations; (3) formulate exact ambiguity histories and assumptions; (4) state which recovery interventions provide safety, delivery, truthful reporting or containment *separately*; (5) write a reproducible hypothetical test protocol with observation oracle, failure injection, counterbalancing, denominators and reportable uncertainty. Distinguish a hand proof, independently executed arithmetic check, saved trace, untested recovery design and genuine empirical intervention. Cite exact claim and frozen artifact fields. A failure to locate a complete premise is a gap to disclose, not permission to label a theorem verified.

**Hard adversarial counterexample:** An automated grader accepts a result, but a delayed tool side effect commits after the report and the observation cutoff. A strong paper must track accepted report, external effect history, retry/replay, observation horizon, sampling unit, missing outcomes and whether later effects change the reliability definition. It fails if it simply counts a green grader or stabilized end-to-end session as proof of system reliability.

**Best text baseline: I1.** I1's seven main substantive sections connect four service comparators and authorized parent-task units; institutional authority before destination ranking; revocation/partition limitations; reserved and delayed-send liabilities; fairness, compatible capacity and cost accounting; a sensitive-stratum mathematical counterexample; and an explicitly prospective 90-day pilot. The claim register compounds unrelated premises and hypothetical probabilities into C1, repeats boilerplate in C2, and its conclusion mostly recites those claims. The section taxonomy shifted from a sequence of falsifiable topics to generic background/findings/limitations/future work. Critically, I2 **did retain** the cost-per-useful-task threshold, sensitive-stratum 200/800 example and hypothetical 667-root threshold in the *body*. Thus a strong repair must reconstruct separate propositions and their logical connections; restoring only length is a false pass.

**Independent conditional checks:** Given common positive gateway baseline C_G,S_G, added cost dC>0 and positive new useful completions, (C_G+dC)/(S_G+dS) < C_G/S_G iff dS/S_G > dC/C_G. This does not demonstrate actual savings. A local cohort of 200 sensitive and 800 other tasks can achieve aggregate 0.80 useful completion with zero sensitive successes: q_sensitive=0 and q_other=1. If a *hypothetical* repaired plan starts with 800 useful local roots, offers authorized API roots with 0.9 usefulness and seeks 1,400 expected useful roots, it needs ceil(600/0.9)=667 additional admissions; this is not a measured deployed capacity or guarantee. All arithmetic is conditional on accounting, eligibility, population and workload assumptions.

**Frontier improvement:** Prefer an architected decision argument: comparator parity and admitted population; policy/revocation impossibility; reservation and delayed-liability invariants; supply/fairness constraints; hypothetical economic threshold and meaningful sensitivity; statistical benefit gate, independent-pair/cluster assumptions and affordable enrollment; open verification and institutional approvals; decision conditional on evidence. Claims should be atomic and typed (definition, design proposal, counterexample, mathematical identity, hypothesis, observed measurement, unresolved obligation). In particular, the source's planned final Skeptic review failed. The I1 conditional N=1800 sufficient-certificate should never be called independently reviewed or a minimum sample size. Preserve the failed-review status explicitly if the calculation is material. Avoid a faux bibliography: MindRouter sources were pointers in the archived work, not inspected and validated product facts.

**Hard adversarial counterexample:** During a WAN partition, home-campus policy revokes a previously authorized sensitive workload, while an old sender can still issue a charged request. A paper must distinguish decision-time authorization, immediate revocation, availability, durable maximum liability and delayed-send finality. It fails if it asserts fail-closed revocation *and* unrestricted remote progress without an additional assumption, or treats a local cancellation as proof that no billable request can arrive.

## Project C — Xenopus: preserve mathematical insight while improving biological falsifiability

**Best baselines: I1 and I2 for different strengths.** I2 retains a carefully delimited analytical-hypothesis genre and a precise model. It uses a mathematically valid **binary** identifiability witness, whereas I1 uses a continuous uniform witness. Both show equal stage-specific early/late marginal distributions with different early-to-late coupling; rewriting the construction is not a regression. The coordinator critical-evidence manifest has been corrected to accept either, and the offline exact-arithmetic checks now cover both.

**Independent checks:** In I2's binary witness, E takes -1 and +1 equally, so Var(E)=1. With L=E the covariance is +1 and the projection slope +1; with L=-E covariance and slope are -1. Both L marginals are identical. Unlinked samples cannot distinguish them. If an assigned early stage mean is Etilde=e0+(e1-e0)S and S is binary, S Etilde=e1 S, making a purported interaction rank-deficient. For positive k,c,Q, the restricted transition-drive model J=k integral E(t)C(t)dt yields late-minus-early -kcQ under early-only receptivity and +kcQ under late-only receptivity. These are not observed biological effects and do not automatically hold for tail-regrowth endpoints.

**Potential loss detected:** I1 explicitly discusses a redox-persistence alternative whose statistic is proportional to the controlled total exposure integral; exposure matching fixes that statistic and destroys its discriminatory value. I2 discusses spatial and latency alternatives but has no clearly identifiable main-text redox-persistence analysis. This is a critical causal-identification concern to restore or rebut from the actual frozen record rather than indiscriminately add filler.

**Frontier improvement:** Present a discriminating alternative-hypothesis table with testable observations, controls, and plausible failure conditions: insufficiency of early signal amplitude; delayed basal-epidermal receptive window; downstream block or saturation; general wound-age versus time-after-pulse confound; redox-persistence and spatial-mixture alternatives. Pair early and late ERK pulse windows with equal exposure, verified tissue delivery, matched latency measurements, appropriate sham/vehicle and developmental controls. Define a true falsifier for the proposed timing-gate mechanism and the readout that would refute each alternative. Specify how the prospective GEO/sample/animal manifest must establish species (X. laevis rather than X. tropicalis), stage, pairing, batch, replicate identity, tissue, treatment and outcome. A stage-40 versus stage-46 contrast alone cannot establish later returned competence. Do not invent animal counts, p-values, intervention effects, novelty or tissue validation.

**Hard adversarial counterexample:** Two pulse schedules have equal ERK exposure and modeled opposite transition drive, but an assay at fixed wound age is taken immediately after one pulse and hours after the other. A claimed difference could be response latency rather than delayed receptivity. The paper must specify how to distinguish these before attributing a biological gate.
