## Executive judgment

**SIGMA's existing research is substantially better than a naive model debate, but it reaches a rigorous, conditional argument more reliably than it reaches decisive independent evidence.** Good examples: the Agent dropped-retry counterexample retracting an overbroad impossibility bound; Sovereignty's revocation/partition indistinguishability and sensitive-stratum counterexample; and Xenopus' mathematically valid marginal-vs-joint nonidentifiability. **Preserve those intellectual gains.**

The highest-impact problems are: (1) decisive source/premise not reliably available to the final Skeptic; (2) correct mathematical conditionals sitting alongside unresolved empirical, legal, causal and implementation assumptions; (3) external starting references and dataset metadata not actually inspected; (4) hypotheses and feasible discriminating next steps not always pushed into a decisive analytical check or narrowed research conclusion; (5) incomplete adversarial reviews that must remain incomplete. These are **multiple independent fronts**, not a single prompt fix.

**Major correction to a simplistic diagnosis:** the current research engine already contains extensive proof-obligation, cross-strategy, citation, computation, counterexample and scientific-method instructions. It also preserves original artifacts and marks bounded extracts. This review does **not** recommend simply adding more generic prompt prose. First measure actual turn behavior and final post-compaction evidence; make small, causal, testable improvements using the existing Investigator/Skeptic loop.

### R1 — Calibrate what a finding means: truth, conditionality, source availability and reviewer agreement

**Area:** evidence quality / scientific uncertainty / depth. **Priority:** P1. **Classification:** C for strong conditional reasoning, S for unverified saved statuses, B where paper labels overstated it. **Confidence:** high about mathematical examples; high about absence of an independent verification certificate.

**Source:** Sovereignty's eight artifacts all partial; no independently verified source status. Its **Investigator step 14** (50147fc3-6e62-4c43-b3b6-68eb3f268965) contains a conditional sufficient-pairs computation that did not receive the intended final skeptic review. Phase 1 paper C18 cited the entire session rather than this exact step. Research code research_epistemics.py explicitly warns that even legacy “verified” is not independent proof. Proof and evidence standards are already present in INVESTIGATOR_GUIDANCE and SKEPTIC_GUIDANCE.

**Scientific weakness:** there are *several independent uncertainties*: proof validity given assumptions; whether input values are measured; whether a source was inspected; whether a skeptic reviewed all inferential steps; whether the conclusion applies to the target population. One approval or arithmetic success cannot collapse these dimensions.

**Better Investigator/Skeptic behavior:** build a small **claim-level** inspection for the hardest 6–10 existing propositions. For each: exact proposition and quantifier; assumptions; step/artefact IDs; support/refutation/qualification relation; full versus truncated source availability; independent computational check; empirical/external support; unresolved strongest objection. Have Skeptic attack the highest-consequence unsupported assumption, and only accept a derivation whose actual argument was in context. Make any new validation lightweight and content-directed rather than a sprawling new schema.

**Counterexample / controls:** Sovereign conditional cost identity ΔS/S_G > ΔC/C_G is valid if positive common baseline denominators are satisfied, even if campus prices and expected S are unknown. The reported n=1800 is a *sufficient* conditional bound, not the smallest sample size or an independently tested experimental estimate: Phase 1 check of its specific Hoeffding/Cantelli inequality yields threshold about **1730.9048**, so n=1731 already satisfies that inequality. Neither statement verifies affordability, clustered trials or implementation. Agent's p=q−f+g is an exact partition identity, but q is not actual p absent directional error allowances. Xenopus ±kcQ belongs to a restricted transition-drive model, not a measured regeneration difference.

**Pass:** improve claim-specific epistemic calibration **without** degrading valid mathematics into generic “possibly false” uncertainty, or upgrading model agreement into external verification. Method: NASEM *Reproducibility and Replicability in Science* chapters 2–3; ACM SIGSOFT Empirical Standards, General and method-specific standards.

### R2 — Investigator/Skeptic should pursue the strongest *discriminating* next step, not simply propose more research

**Area:** missed opportunity / depth / methods / skepticism. **Priority:** P1. **Classification:** C+U, with several detailed unresolved obligations visible in manuscripts. **Confidence:** medium about unmet research need; low-to-medium that any particular candidate test was technically available in the frozen session.

**Observed → weakness:** all three bodies generate reasonable analytical candidates, but unresolved alternatives and external conditions often remain. A future experiment outline is not evidence. A second strategy repeating the leading story is not a test.

**Better behavior:** using *existing* investigation.method, reasoning_steps, open_obligations, reviewer objections and available execution tools, pick one highest-value uncertain proposition and declare: (1) leading hypotheses/interpretations, (2) assumption under which they differ, (3) the single most feasible observation, exact derivation, counterexample or computation that discriminates, (4) outcome-specific update to each claim, (5) what remains impossible with current source/tools. Skeptic must challenge the discrimination itself (confounding, alternative source, omitted premise, inappropriate population), not just ask for a “stronger study.”

**Tests:** can one more independent piece of evidence falsify the preferred explanation? Does the observation distinguish models, or is it identical under them? If no feasible discriminator exists, the correct result is a sharp impossibility or unresolved condition—not a fabricated experiment.

**Method:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy *Abduction*, especially §1 (hypothesis selection need not logically imply truth) and §3 (normative status/criticisms); NIST/SEMATECH Engineering Statistics Handbook §5.3.3 (comparative vs screening vs response-surface designs); Center for Open Science *Preregistration* (exploration vs later confirmation). Existing comprehensive prompts already mention discriminating outcomes: **evaluate adherence before adding text.**

### R3 — Agent reliability: extend the excellent counterexample work into an executable observation model

**Area:** depth / rigor / original analysis / applied testing. **Priority:** P1. **Classification:** M+A for observed analytical strengths and proposed tests; E for absence of complete original source trace audit. **Confidence:** high for the conditional derivations, medium for identifying the strongest next research action.

**Must preserve:** artifact a1a9ab51-f8d1-4b60-ba10-baad7585a34f:984e8fd3-f76b-46fb-b868-378c2f103425 describes the ambiguous-acknowledgment model. **Remaining hard question:** under which agent-visible histories and *precisely which retry-delivery, acknowledgment, effect-commit, checkpoint and idempotency contracts* is a guarantee possible or impossible? The bound max(r,1−r)≥1/2 requires an **assured-commit retry** in the simplified indistinguishable two-history model; allowing dropped retries breaks the proposed generalization. “Exactly once” must specify the external effect and payload, not merely client-send or local-key insertion. Model agent-supplied p=q−f+g and task-roster versus independent task-cluster population as separate estimands.

**Skeptic test:** find one history with the same agent observation but different external truth, and one case where a supposed general guarantee fails because a contract assumption changed. ### R5 — Xenopus: test whether competing mechanisms are *identifiable* under proposed observations

**Area:** causal method / alternative mechanisms / scientific evidence / novelty. **Priority:** P1. **Classification:** M+A for analytical discoveries; U+E for actual specimen, GEO and literature evidence. **Confidence:** high for algebraic nonidentifiability; unverified for biological truth or source-turn behavior.

**Preserve:** a valid **continuous** witness E=U,L=U vs E=U,L=1−U, or **binary** witness E∈{−1,+1}, L=E vs −E: same marginals, opposite slopes +1/−1. This proves a *specific lack of individual-level identification* from unpaired marginals, not that all population contrasts are useless. Assigned stage-mean interaction S Etilde=e1 S adds no independent variation. Under the **restricted model** J=k∫E(t)C(t)dt, equal-exposure early/late pulses can yield ±kcQ with assumed k,c,Q>0; this is a model contrast, not observed tail rescue.

**The critical scientific decision:** can a matched-exposure pulse distinguish delayed receptivity from insufficient early signal, downstream blockage, redox persistence, spatial mixture, or assay-response latency? At fixed wound age, late-pulsed tissue is observed closer to its intervention than early-pulsed tissue; post-pulse latency is a confound. Also, if a proposed redox-persistence metric is proportional to *the same matched total exposure integral*, exposure matching necessarily equalizes that metric—so that **metric cannot distinguish** the mechanisms. Another independent redox or temporal measurement would be needed.

**Better Investigator:** start with a source-verified biological unit and paired/unpaired data design. For GEO candidates GSE245320, GSE245317, GSE245318, GSE245312 and GSE245313, inspect a manifest with species, stage, actual tissue/cells, biological animals/pools, replicate, batch, pairing, assay and phenotype; do not pretend to have downloaded matrices from an accession pointer. Design prospective equal-dose pulses with independently matched wound-age and post-pulse-latency readouts, tissue delivery checks, sham/vehicle, downstream-state markers and distinct alternative predictions. Specify what *negative observation* genuinely falsifies the timing-gate hypothesis under validated delivery and readout. Do not treat stage 40→46 as evidence of later returned competence; do not transfer X. tropicalis results silently to X. laevis.

**Better Skeptic:** seek an observationally equivalent alternative and demand a measurement that breaks the equivalence. Check whether the supposed proposed experiment has an identifiability failure even if executed perfectly. Check relevant prior biological literature novelty only after actually retrieving it; avoid claims of animal interventions without actual data.

**Expected gain:** a narrower but legitimately falsifiable mechanism and credible source/assay prerequisites, with no invented regeneration effect or sample sizes. Methods: NIST design matching objective and factors; NIH/NC3Rs experimental-unit and allocation guidance when *actual* animal design is contemplated; causal identification and confounding principles (Hernán–Robins, applicable sections only when read); SEP abduction on alternatives.

## Independent scientific calculations and non-results to preserve

1. Agent: q=P(O=1), f=P(O=1,Y=0), g=P(O=0,Y=1), p=P(Y=1) gives exactly **p=q−f+g**. The restricted, assured-commit two-history retry minimax error is at least 1/2; this does **not** transfer to arbitrary lost/dropped retries.
2. Sovereign: with C_G,S_G>0 and permitted increments, **(C_G+ΔC)/(S_G+ΔS)<C_G/S_G** iff **ΔS/S_G>ΔC/C_G**, provided S_G+ΔS>0. This is not economic evidence that the hypothetical federation saves money. Sensitive 200/other 800 can produce aggregate 0.8 useful completion with **zero** sensitive success in a constructed case. n=1800 satisfies one conditional sufficient inequality, not a measured enrollment or universal minimum.
3. Xenopus: two equal unpaired marginals can have opposite joint slopes. The restricted ±kcQ contrast does not prove an animal phenotype. Matched exposure also fixes any redox statistic proportional solely to that exposure, so it cannot discriminate the proposed mechanisms.

**A false research evaluation would penalize these as “unsupported” in the same way as an invented empirical result.** Instead check exact premises and report the strongest valid bounded result.
