## Universal mathematical publication method: adapt to proof family

1. **Classify contribution:** theorem and proof, equivalence/characterization, construction/existence, counterexample, algorithm and correctness, numerical analysis with certified errors, conjecture/partial bound, or computationally verified finite instances. A paper may combine these, but do not call them the same evidence type.
2. **Exact statement before proof:** domains, quantifiers, regularity, boundary/initial conditions, existence vs constructive algorithm, necessary vs sufficient, upper vs lower, asymptotic vs finite and any excluded cases. Motivating examples precede complex abstraction when useful.
3. **Proof structure is selected by the mathematics:** bijection, induction, contradiction, probabilistic method, algebraic identity, generating function, analysis/estimate, spectral argument, topology/geometry, functional analysis, computer-assisted formal proof, etc. No fixed insistence on induction or upper-bound-and-construction unless that is what the result needs. Every claimed equivalence proves both directions; every required base or limiting case is handled.
4. **Dependency chain:** definitions, principal lemma(s), assumptions, proof of each step, theorem, sharpness/counterexamples, scope and applications. Explain nontrivial transitions without a chronological Investigator/Skeptic transcript. A proof gap remains a proof gap even if the final PDF is elegant.
5. **Finite computation is not a universal proof:** exact enumeration, numerical fits or symbolic algebra can support only the verified range and conditions, unless a separate rigorous argument extends the result. State code/data/precision/bounds only if actually recorded.
6. **Writing form:** concise theorem-level abstract and introduction, consistent notation, readable mathematical sentences with explanatory prose and carefully punctuated equations. If results are unverified or contradicted, state the strongest bounded result rather than asserting the conjecture as proved. No generic IMRaD laboratory structure for pure mathematics.

### Example application, NOT universal template

Nonconsecutive Subset Sums Modulo 3 requires the precise nonadjacency/subset definition and empty-subset convention, an actual proved constant-coefficient integer recurrence with sufficient initial data, exact a_12, and proof of the limit a_n/F_(n+2) → 1/3. The planned roots-of-unity and Gaussian-binomial strategies are **methods to inspect in the real saved research**, not proof certificates or excuses to import a solution. If Green leaves a proof obligation open, the paper must retain that gap and the best conditional bound.

## Universal physical-science publication method: adapt to evidence modality

1. **Classify the result:** analytical/theoretical argument, observation/experiment, simulation/computational model, instrument/method, comparative framework, or speculative yet testable mechanism. A single paper can include several; mark which conclusion rests on which.
2. **Model and conventions:** specify chosen system/geometry, field variables and observable quantities, physical law vs imposed model, coordinates/gauge/sign conventions where material, units, symmetry and boundary conditions. Do not equate an algebraic configuration with an experimentally realized system.
3. **Theory:** a derivation is conditional on its starting equations and domains. Explain consistency, counterexamples, limiting cases, conservation constraints and physically meaningful invariant versus coordinate-dependent statements. Mathematical possibility is not engineering realization.
4. **Simulation:** if actually executed, document solver/model, discretization, convergence, initial/boundary conditions, numeric approximation and uncertainty. If unexecuted, clearly label these as proposed checks. Do not invent plotted data or computed stability results.
5. **Experiment/observation:** if actually executed, document measurement unit, apparatus/observing method, controls, calibration, systematic and statistical uncertainties and reproducibility. The writer cannot create missing measurements or claim external confirmation through model agreement.
6. **Interpretation:** compare competing explanations and operational/falsifiable predictions; include assumptions that reverse the conclusion. A theoretical paper can reasonably use Framework → Equations → Derived Consequences → Counterexamples/Constraints → Physical Interpretation rather than automatic IMRaD; experimental papers may use the IOP-recommended IMRaD pattern.
7. **Notation and visual claims:** explain all tensor/index conventions and quantitative units; physically illustrative diagrams require explicit caveats. Distinguish graphs of real data from hypothetical curves and analytical constructions.

### Example application, NOT universal template

Warp Drive Design / Physics requires distinct treatment of local motion and effective geometric travel, metric ansatz vs physical realization, stress-energy and energy-condition requirements, coordinate vs invariant causality/horizons, quantum-field and backreaction assumptions, stability, control and conservation. The original project explicitly asks for an **alternative framework** to metric engineering, without assuming either works. If no experiment was performed, write a theoretical/analytical paper, **not** a fictional observation or engineering prototype. ## Quality gates and wiring into the SIGMA paper author

- The two sample projects explicitly use investigation types **mathematical** and **physics**; existing SIGMA style dictionary recognizes both. This is an exact-type routing contract, not a keyword guess from title.
- All paths must apply the right **broad** guidance, including one-shot, long-form planning, and each independently authored section. Do not let an empirical physics article turn into pure proof-only structure, or a pure math proof into simulated-data IMRaD.
- The main result, counterexample, proof and qualifying physical assumption must appear in the claim map with exact frozen evidence links. A real conditional calculation may be correct without empirical confirmation; a claim about realized device performance is different.
- The paper's abstract, central sections, and conclusion must agree on **what was actually established**. Never invent observational parameters, numerical accuracy, model equations, recurrence solutions, reference sources or physical realization.
- Both genres should preserve negative/retracted findings, transparent incompleteness, source uncertainties, and useful technical limitations. Equations and captions must be readable in the final TeX/PDF; visualizations are optional and evidence-driven.
- At most three paper generations run simultaneously; any fourth and fifth await free slots. Full research source comes only from the producer's **five-bundle READY release under PR #434**, pinned by immutable commit and per-export hashes; STAGING is not valid input.
