### Sovereignty: verified findings vs overclaim and omissions

1. **Conditional truths retained:** The proposed zero-additional-usable-peer-capacity case establishes only a conditional gateway advantage, not a universal anti-federation conclusion. The useful-task threshold compares **authorized, rubric-passing parent-task completions**, not raw calls. With positive baseline denominators and comparable cost scope, the conditional inequality `ΔS/S_G > ΔC/C_G` can be exact without any measured campus benefit. Under disconnected revocation with no fresh authoritative status, a cached authorization cannot guarantee both immediate revocation and unrestricted progress. Do not turn a design-time impossibility into proof about the actual MindRouter implementation.
2. **Consequence-rich arithmetic:** The example with 200 sensitive admissions requires a sensitive usefulness rate of at least 0.8 to obtain expected 160 successful sensitive roots. The I3 manuscript correctly distinguishes actual admitted sensitive count from the hypothetical 200. Aggregate 800 successes can coexist with zero sensitive usefulness when another group absorbs the successes; the manuscript must preserve this alternative and its conditional nature. One illustrative workload calculation is not campus measurement.
3. **Clear incompleteness missing from body:** Phase 1 independently inspected the saved Sovereignty after-research bundle. Its **call 18 Fairness/Skeptic failed**, leaving an incomplete review. The I3 prose says obligations are unresolved and literature uninspected, but a direct search of the complete I3 TXT shows **no explicit "failed turn", no "research is incomplete", and no description of the call-18 failure**. This is more specific than general evidence uncertainty: intended critical scrutiny was never completed. A new code path now derives a completeness limitation from the original session state, without claiming prior analytical results are false. The new release must show the failed-review status.
4. **Source provenance boundary:** MindRouter primary sources were not retrieved in the frozen research. Our independent post hoc reading of the documentation and PEARC paper cannot be retroactively attributed to SIGMA research. Claims about financial guarantees from generic quotas are particularly unsafe: the official documentation notes rate-limiting behavior that fails open on Redis loss. Maintain a distinction between maintainer-reported capabilities, exact externally verified operations and hypothetical federation extensions.
5. **Argument structure failure:** The I3 `Conclusions` paragraphs are 3,869 characters in total and contain a pasted 2,000+ character block of unrelated MindRouter feature statements and source availability. The opening `Abstract` elevates a narrow sensitive-gate arithmetic sub-result over the main original question, while the body argues a much broader policy-before-capacity position. This is editorial failure, not absence of that research.
6. **Epistemic types collapsed:** The 22-register items all read "Unresolved", regardless of whether the statement is (i) stipulation/comparator, (ii) conditional theorem, (iii) counterexample, (iv) unverified external feature, (v) open evidence obligation. A safe next-generation register needs source kind and verification distinction without upgrading theoretical deductions to measured results. The fallback now records source-index classification as a **type label**, not an independent proof verdict. Do not change established status indiscriminately.
7. **TeX:** Lines around the register's source path and `n_G,local,S` produce overfull 59pt and 193pt boxes in Blue's logs. ### Xenopus: strong model reasoning, deficient claim coverage

1. **The main proof remains valid under its premise:** The I3 paper explicitly constructs stage-conditioned equal marginal distributions with opposite animal-level coupling: with `Z∈{-1,+1}`, `E=μ_s+Z`, `L=ν_s±Z`, the within-stage linear projection slopes are ±1 and the observed unpaired marginals coincide. Interchanging signs across stages changes the cross-stage slope contrast by ±2 without changing the marginals. This **does not** show that published GEO data are actually unpaired; that provenance remains uninspected.
2. **Stage-mean aliasing:** `Etilde=e0+(e1-e0)S` and `S Etilde=e1 S` imply a rank≤2 design, so assigning the stage mean to every late animal cannot recover individual response interaction. This is a mathematical warning about wrong biological units, not a new measured effect.
3. **Proposed pulse test is sharply conditional:** The restricted cumulative drive `J=k∫E(t)C(t)dt` yields opposite signs `−kcQ` and `+kcQ` for equal-integral pulses only if the receptive supports are appropriately separated, `k,c,Q>0` and intervention does not alter receptivity. It is **not a measured biological rescue or a general theorem about all alternative mechanisms**. The I3 text acknowledges overlap, downstream block, saturation and the need to match achieved rather than nominal ERK exposure.
4. **Actual improved I3 writing:** The text distinguishes fixed wound-age readouts from latency since treatment, explicitly defines `τ=t−t_p`, requests matched-latency and matched-wound-age series, companion specimens, vehicle corrections and controls for cell abundance, and conditions any mechanistic inference on assay coverage and target engagement. These improvements are real and must survive future shorter papers.
5. **Redox lost:** The I3 body briefly mentions prior ROS signaling, but does not state the decisive **exposure-matched redox-persistence null**: if the metric is proportional to exactly the integrated exposure held fixed between arms, that metric cannot discriminate the two timing hypotheses. This particular limitation was developed in earlier papers and must be restored from the research source or correctly marked unsupported if the new frozen research changes.
6. **Core claim register failure:** The proof, stage-mean alias and ±kcQ derivation occupy substantive I3 body paragraphs but are not independent claim-register entries. Three of the earliest main sections lack linked claim IDs. The five registered claims are C10/C11/C12 and verbatim repeats C1/C2 of C11/C12. TeX itself lists a source validation warning that `C01..C09` were absent; those are not a valid substitute for a consistent register. **Novel mathematical content is not made more credible by a small, convenient bibliography.**
7. **Remaining status:** No inspected GEO sample manifest, expression matrices, animal-level linkage, treatment effect, independent biological validation, verified later-competent X. laevis state, or established novelty. One of the genuinely strongest features is the paper's restraint. Preserve it while strengthening the argument map.
