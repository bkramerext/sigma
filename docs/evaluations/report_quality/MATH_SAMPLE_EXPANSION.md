## Mathematics-specific sources — reviewed links and concrete use

1. **Flajolet & Sedgewick, _Analytic Combinatorics_, author booksite**: https://sedgewick.io/books/analytic-combinatorics/ — table of contents and online reference for symbolic structures, rational/meromorphic methods, transfer-matrix models (chapter V), and asymptotic coefficient methods (chapters IV–VI). Princeton overview https://ac.cs.princeton.edu/home/ states book is freely downloadable, but direct automated access can be restricted; author booksite is accessible. Use only verified chapters/excerpts actually read; distinguish mathematical theorem from booksite summary.
2. **Stanford CS103, _Proofwriting Checklist_**, full readable HTML: https://web.stanford.edu/class/archive/cs/cs103/cs103.1262/proofwriting_checklist — assumptions and exact want-to-prove, scoped variables/quantifiers, load-bearing steps and reasons. Applicable as independent proof-quality checks, not a requirement to mimic undergraduate wording.
3. **Bertsekas, _Ten Simple Rules for Mathematical Writing_**, detailed accessible HTML outline: https://faculty.engineering.asu.edu/bertsekas/ten-simple-rules-for-mathematical-writing/ — segment hierarchy, consistent notation, appropriate examples/counterexamples, no under- or over-explanation.
4. **Knuth, Larrabee & Roberts, _Mathematical Writing_**, Stanford author page https://cs.stanford.edu/~knuth/klr.html — 31-lecture mathematical-writing text is available as compressed TeX with accessible overview; don't claim the complete text has been read without retrieving it.
5. **MIT, Mathematics for Computer Science**, public lecture notes: https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-1200j-mathematics-for-computer-science-spring-2024/resources/lecture-notes/ — proof techniques, induction and recurrences; consult exact lecture/material as relevant.
6. **AMS official `amsthm` documentation**: https://www.ams.org/arc/tex/amscls/amsthdoc.pdf — official theorem, lemma, conjecture and proof typesetting conventions. PDF source; visual inspection and exact-source checks are separate from scientific acceptance.
7. **Bjorn Poonen, _Practical Suggestions for Mathematical Writing_**: https://math.mit.edu/~poonen/papers/writing.pdf — precise claims, links between assumptions and conclusions, unambiguous quantifiers, shorter lemmas, localized citations; PDF is freely posted, selectively read for review.
8. **SIAM / Nicholas Higham, _It's a Matter of Style_**: https://www.siam.org/publications/siam-news/articles/it-s-a-matter-of-style/ — style is secondary to valid technical content; mathematics exposition remains a scientific argument, not an empirical study.

Use links as traceable references, not verbatim prompt dumps. Do not claim to have inspected full PDF/book text when only landing/abstract was available.

## Phase 1 paper author's mathematics-specific acceptance contract

**Same frozen research, improved mathematical exposition**. Do not re-run Investigator/Skeptic in Phase 1. Main paper should resemble a mathematical note, *not* forced IMRaD empirical sections:
- State exact definition of `a_n`, domain/indexing/empty subset, foundational generating polynomial, target theorem(s), and proof status. Introduce notation before use.
- Distinguish **proved lemma/proposition/theorem**, **conditional statement**, **conjecture**, **finite exact computation**, **failed argument/refuted recurrence**, and **unresolved proof obligation**. No false 'we prove' for a finite fit or model hypothesis.
- Organize a coherent sequence: definition → key encodings and lemmas → provable finite result → attempted global recurrence with exact missing proof step → asymptotic density and precise unresolved spectral-growth step → conclusions/open problems. Structure should reflect the *actual saved evidence*; do not present an unproved target as a theorem merely to fit the outline.
- For candidate recurrence: show full integer coefficients, valid index range, justified sufficient initial values and complete derivation if actually available. Fitting first 60 terms ≠ proof. Check phase-ordered matrix multiplication, characteristic polynomial and base range; accept minimal/general recurrences only when justified, avoid asserting minimal order without proof.
- For density limit: a global recurrence by itself does not prove equidistribution. Require an explicit bound for nontrivial root-of-unity contributions, with spectral/rate assumptions justified, or state this as unproved.
- For Gaussian/q-binomial expression, preserve correct exponent, degree, boundary and normalization; retain failed variants/repair provenance. Typeset mathematical commands safely without leaking escaped literal TeX, maintain exact equations, and fix missing claim references. No fabricated citations or formal verification claims.
- Clear precise proof prose, not repeated metadata or bloated general 'Methods/Results'; source provenance can be in a compact appendix but never conceal uncertainty. Avoid forced biological/engineering rubrics.
- Compare **complete TXT/TeX/PDF** from identical frozen session snapshots on controlled I4 authoring settings. Judge proof correctness and completeness separately from TeX render fidelity, claim-link coverage, length and style. Visually inspect PDF equations only after verifying they match authored TeX.
