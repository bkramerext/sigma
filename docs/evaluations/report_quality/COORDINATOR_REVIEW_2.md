## What I independently inspected

I read the complete available 9,518-word Sovereignty TXT manuscript, including its abstract, seven substantive authored sections, 19-item claim register, limitations, open obligations and conclusions. A deterministic text pass identified **19** register items (`C1`–`C19`), **55** occurrences of bracketed `[C#]` claim pointers, **four** URL occurrences (the same two starting source URLs repeated), and **15** blank-line-delimited TXT blocks. These are *textual counts*, not independent proof of source support or PDF fidelity. The 42-page PDF and its TeX are present in the PR; no full visual PDF audit was performed in this turn. Earlier independent review found unresolved overflow and proofreading failures.

**Finding A — the current register collapses epistemically distinct meanings into `established`.** Six examples:
- **C2 and C3:** `established` denotes *stipulated comparison alternatives and a definition* of useful parent-task completion. These are definitions, not findings about real campus services.
- **C11:** `established` denotes a **conditional algebraic identity**. The manuscript correctly derives `ΔS/S_G > ΔC/C_G` from positive denominators and fixed cost basis. It is not empirical evidence of federation savings.
- **C13:** `established` denotes a **hand-worked counterexample** to a general implication; it is not measured sensitive-stratum behavior.
- **C14:** `established` denotes **arithmetic under imposed hypothetical probabilities**: `800 + 0.9n` expected completions and `n ≥ 667` for a hypothetical 1,400/2,000 target. It must not be read as observed completion or guaranteed sensitive-task coverage.
- **C15:** `established` denotes **conditional dominance** when federation adds cost but no usable service; it is not a measured result over a realistic deployment.

The manuscript frequently explains these limitations correctly in the body, but a reader scanning its claim register sees six different epistemic categories collapsed under the same word. **The needed fix is typed claims plus separate verification status, not blanket downgrading of sound conditional reasoning.**

**Finding B — `conjecture` also conflates distinct objects.** C4 and C8 are **design proposals**, C5 and C6 are **conditional proof candidates** with unverified premises, C16 is a **proposed decision rule**, and C18 is a **reported but not fully independently reproduced numerical certificate**. Treating them all as `conjecture` impairs reviewer triage. Use `claim_kind` (definition / algebraic deduction / hand counterexample / hypothetical worksheet / proposal / conditional proof candidate / empirical observation / unresolved) separately from `verification` (saved agent assertion / independently checked conditional calculation / source-verified / implemented and tested / not verified). Preserve original claim text and its saved source IDs.

**Finding C — traceability is a substantive blocker, not a citation-style issue.** The `[C#]` pointers link arguments to an internal claim register; they are not bibliographic references or proof that an external source supports a statement. The only two named external MindRouter starting sources were explicitly **not retrieved** in the underlying analysis. Four URL occurrences do not mean four distinct references. Do not promote protocol support, WDRR or telemetry descriptions to independently confirmed product capabilities. A future external-source enrichment experiment must be separate from a frozen-evidence editorial A/B and must record access date, precise quotation/claim and source version.

**Finding D — the evidence truncation metric needs a better denominator.** The generator reportedly selected 372 fragments and omitted 9,718 saved **field fragments**. Those are not 9,718 distinct papers, claims or necessarily consequential facts. A better, falsifiable coverage metric: enumerate **decision-critical items** from the frozen record (strongest counterexamples, failed Skeptic turn, contradictory artifacts, newest corrected claims, unresolved high-severity obligations), and measure which are represented faithfully in the manuscript with source pointers. Treat count of excluded fields as a **coverage-risk flag**, not as a scientifically meaningful percent of evidence omitted. The failed Sovereign turn 18 (`Skeptic cited obligation evidence not selected into its context`) is a specific high-priority candidate for such an audit.

**Finding E — the 90-day efficacy gate must be interpreted conditionally.** The earlier coordinator's exact finite-distribution calculation found `n=1800` has ~0.999983 pass probability under the specified IID hypothetical `Δ=0.20,q=0.40`, but ~0.4632 for `Δ=0.15,q=0.80`. This does not invalidate the manuscript's *conditional* certificate; it invalidates any unqualified enrollment recommendation. Parent tasks repeated within researchers, shared queues and time windows also threaten independence. An acceptable paper needs a clearly labeled **sensitivity/feasibility paragraph** and a pre-specified clustered/blocked design if a real pilot is proposed. Never inject these independently computed results into the frozen baseline and then claim the generator discovered them.

**Finding F — length is not evidence of completeness.** The TXT has only 15 blank-line-delimited blocks despite 9,518 words, while the TeX reportedly has finer paragraph structure. Evaluate text and TeX separately. The main manuscript is diluted by 19 claim-register items, raw research excerpts and diagnostic sections; check whether a tighter core argument plus linked appendices improves reader comprehension without hiding counterexamples or unresolved obligations. Do not shorten until claim/evidence coverage is secured.

## Two additional academic-writing references reviewed this turn

**ACM SIGSOFT [Empirical Standards](https://www2.sigsoft.org/EmpiricalStandards/docs/standards), Engineering Research and Longitudinal Studies:** The engineering-research standard distinguishes conceptual analysis of an invented artifact from empirical evaluation and requires clear comparison or an explicit rationale why comparison is impractical. The longitudinal standard requires subject identity across waves, attrition/missing-data accounting and methods appropriate for dependence. Apply these as **conditional reviewer criteria** to the AI Agent manuscript: if it is purely a conceptual design, do not invent benchmark results; if it claims longitudinal reliability, require run-level traceability and non-independence handling.

**Baethge, Goldbeck-Wood & Mertens, [SANRA](https://doi.org/10.1186/s41073-019-0064-8), *Research Integrity and Peer Review* 4:5 (2019):** A six-item quality-assessment instrument for **narrative** reviews, emphasizing importance, aims, search description, references, scientific reasoning/evidence level and endpoint data. Its original validation setting was medical narrative reviews; it is **not** a universal publication checklist or a guarantee of scientific truth. For Xenopus, borrow the questions about how literature was found, whether contrary studies are represented and whether experimental endpoint data are faithfully described; do not assign a spurious SANRA total to a non-review research proposal.
