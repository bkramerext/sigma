## What changed

The Skeptic instruction formerly demanded a complete argument be rewritten on every cycle, immediately before saying earlier artifacts could satisfy an omitted requirement. The corrected instruction asks the Skeptic to compare the current delta with **actually supplied** saved steps, cite their exact artifact ID and supporting cycle, request only missing derivation when assumptions/scope change or an objection invalidates a step, and keep unresolved duties visible. The full static [baseline](SKEPTIC_GUIDANCE_BASELINE.txt) and [corrected](SKEPTIC_GUIDANCE_CORRECTED.txt) text are preserved. This removes the blanket rewrite request; it does not prove any model will reliably follow the instruction.

Later sessions now receive bounded `reasoning_steps` including the saved argument and its review, with explicit truncation flags. The earlier compact index had a claim, method, objections and provenance but not the actual step argument. A model can now inspect the supplied derivation before citing it. The record format and old artifacts are unchanged. A new Skeptic closure check rejects an `addressed` obligation based solely on an absent, retracted, or reasoning-truncated older artifact. A usable older basis must contain supplied reasoning steps; an accepted current Investigator step remains another possible basis. This is a **source integrity check**, not a mathematical proof verifier. A plausible but wrong complete step can still be accepted by scripted or live roles; status remains model reported and unverified.
