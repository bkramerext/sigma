## Honest outcome

**The strong practical win is research integrity and cumulative orchestration, not comparative performance.** We have an actual bounded example of a Skeptic identifying and resolving a material proof gap. We also have deterministic counterfactual examples showing saved evidence and real computation alter the subsequent research request. No archived apples-to-apples single-role comparison exists for those cases. No score difference, significance, savings or superiority claim is justified.

The original Increment 15 hypothesis is **not tested**. Declining paid inference does not constitute a negative result for SIGMA. It rules out running the planned experimental design with new paid requests. It does not rule out the possibility of free, already-accounted-for existing sessions being compared retrospectively, but only if their exact inputs, model, budgets and outcome evidence are independently verifiable.
