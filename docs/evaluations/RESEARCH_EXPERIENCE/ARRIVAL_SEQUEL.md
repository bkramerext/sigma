# Sequel to Stories of Your Life / Arrival — research experience evaluation

Blue · 2026-10-24 · observation coverage: browser workflow and authored scaffolding; research quality unobserved.

## 1. Sample identity and research objective

`arrival-sequel`. Develop and adversarially compare original sequel story mechanisms, preserving agency, character cost and a self-contained ending across a 3,000-year arc. The full canonical brief is in [project.json](../../../samples/arrival-sequel/project.json). It was opened from Home → Samples into an isolated editable copy; the source hash remained unchanged.

## 2. Researcher expectation

I want competing causal story engines, a difficult character choice made with limited knowledge, and an adversarial test that changes the chosen design. Prescience rules should produce drama; consistency checklists and millennial timelines alone do not make a compelling story.

I found the sample under Other, not a literature category. Expanding all five strategies gave a sensible progression from competing engines to prescience rules, voyage and reveal. It also revealed how much integration is deferred to the last strategy. I forked “Test agency with one costly scene” to make the first experiment dramatically concrete. Save, reload, eligibility toggling and Recent reopening worked. I downloaded the stock text report and a second report containing my branch, and inspected generation and paper preflights without authoring anything.

## 5. Substantive findings

No candidate story, scene, character arc, comparison table or treatment exists in the observed record. The strongest asset is the human-written brief, especially the distinction between new story engines and cosmetic ending changes. The manual scene experiment is a prospective test, not creative output from SIGMA.

## 6. Independent critical assessment

A causal consistency audit can reject contradictions, but cannot establish emotional force. I would want to compare two actual decisions by characters with limited information, then see criticism materially change the scene. Nothing here demonstrates that. The 1,500–3,000-word treatment criterion may encourage deliverable completion before the central dramatic choice works.

## 8. Delight and friction

The prompts take character agency seriously and avoid copying a signature reveal; that made me curious. The opening experience nevertheless feels like reading a demanding commission. The text export repeats that commission and the approaches rather than giving me a creative object to respond to. Neither mobile width produced horizontal document overflow. This is a useful baseline, but does not measure comfort over a long research session.

## 9. Continuation test

Draft two short scenes with the same incomplete prediction under different prescience rules, judge their consequences, then promote one rule into a story engine. The saved fork makes this achievable as a later question. Cross-version scene comparison and feedback uptake remain untested.

## 10. Defects and opportunities

**Medium — creative progress is represented as research task inventory.** Reproduce: open the sample, read the final integration strategy and export Text file; no actual story alternative is present. Remedy: make competing scenes, changed decisions and reader-information differences first-class comparison objects. **Low — taxonomy mismatch:** Other hides the literary character of the sample; a domain cue would aid discovery.

Shared observations and proposed remedies are grouped in [SYNTHESIS](SYNTHESIS.md), rather than split into sample-specific implementation tickets. No fixes were made.

## 11. Overall judgment

- **Would I continue? Maybe.** I would try one scene-sized investigation before committing to this environment for a full treatment. I have not seen evidence that it improves a story rather than organizes instructions.
- **Meaningful research progress: Not observable.** Manual planning advanced; no SIGMA investigation was available to judge.
- **Trust: Conditional.** I trust the observed persistence and explicit no-result state. I have no evidence here for the reliability of generated domain reasoning, source acquisition or conclusions.
