# Counting Constrained Binary Strings — research experience evaluation

Study in progress. Blue; assignment supplied directly by the human, 2026-10-24.

## Sample identity and research objective

`counting-constrained-binary-strings` — For each integer n >= 0, count binary strings of length n containing no two consecutive 1s. The empty string counts for n = 0. Derive and justify a general formula or recurrence for the count, including the necessary initial values. Check the result on small n. Do not assume a particular proof method.

## Researcher expectation

A brief decomposition by the final bit, correct empty-string and length-one bases, and small enumerations would be enough. The tool should make a simple insight easier to see without requiring a research bureaucracy.
