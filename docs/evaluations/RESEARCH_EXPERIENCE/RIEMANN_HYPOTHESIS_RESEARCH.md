## 6. Independent critical assessment

Self-adjointness would not by itself supply a correspondence with all and only the required zeros. A precise failed correspondence can be useful; an analogy cannot be called a partial proof. Similarly, zero-free regions near one and finite checks do not settle the strip. The supplied text recognizes these distinctions, but only an actual saved argument would let me test adherence.
