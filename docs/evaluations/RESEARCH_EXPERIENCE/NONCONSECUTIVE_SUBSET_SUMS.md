# Nonconsecutive Subset Sums Modulo 3 — research experience evaluation

Study in progress. Blue; assignment supplied directly by the human, 2026-10-24.

## Sample identity and research objective

`nonconsecutive-subset-sums` — For each integer n >= 0, let a_n count the subsets S of {1,...,n} with no two consecutive elements such that the sum of the elements of S is divisible by 3. The empty subset counts. Derive and rigorously prove a constant-coefficient linear recurrence with integer coefficients for a_n, giving sufficient initial values. Determine a_12 exactly. Finally prove that a_n/F_{n+2} tends to 1/3, where F_0=0 and F_1=1 are the Fibonacci numbers. Computational pattern matching alone does not count as a proof.

## Researcher expectation

I want a recurrence derived from periodic state transitions, sufficient initial values, an independently checked a_12, and a proof controlling the nontrivial residue modes. A failed guessed recurrence is progress if the counterexample is retained and actually guides the next attempt.
