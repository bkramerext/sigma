This was one of the strongest opening briefs: a provocative question becomes a disciplined inquiry without preselecting an answer. The loss of momentum is immediate when the apparently research-ready routes have no source objects behind them. Neither mobile width produced horizontal document overflow. This is a useful baseline, but does not measure comfort over a long research session.
