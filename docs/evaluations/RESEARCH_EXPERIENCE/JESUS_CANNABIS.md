## Sample identity and research objective

`jesus-cannabis` — Investigate whether the historical Jesus used cannabis, for any purpose. Evaluate direct and indirect evidence from early texts, historical languages, archaeology, and first-century social practices. Distinguish whether cannabis existed in the region, whether it was used by some people, and whether there is credible evidence that Jesus personally used it. Do not assume either answer; identify uncertainty and explain what evidence would change the conclusion.

## Researcher expectation

I want a source ledger that separates regional availability, community practice and individual attribution, and that distinguishes ancient testimony from repeated modern claims. An honest not-established result with a precise missing link is useful; confident inference from an unrelated place or period is not.
