# Is SIGMA becoming a research environment people would value?

Blue · 2026-10-24 · app revision `6059d67` · independent product/research study.

**SIGMA shows a credible foundation for inspectable research, but this study does not establish a delightful end-to-end live research experience.** The strongest material helps a person understand why an argument failed and what repaired it. The dominant weakness is that preserved records do not reliably become a clear, current intellectual state. A researcher can find the pieces, but must often reconcile the conclusion, evidence, objections and next step themselves.

This is a narrower and more defensible verdict than either “the agents work” or “the product fails.” No live provider work was authorized. Sixteen of the 17 stock samples have no saved research sessions. One contains five explicitly deterministic fixtures. A supplementary, unmodified historical Maximum Regions provider export supplies a second substantive journey. Its original argument is convincing after inspection, but current UI projection makes it harder to recognize.

Desktop was 1440×1000; 390×844 and 320×700 emulation showed no document-width overflow. These are real headless Chrome observations, not physical-phone or sustained human-usability trials.

No research session, provider inference or paper authoring was submitted during the study. Study provider calls and charges are zero. Stored fixture call counts are not real usage; the earlier Maximum Regions plan-use metrics are historical and not independently audited billing. AI generation quality, current live Investigator/Skeptic behavior, active-session intervention, pause/resume/stop, actual source ingestion, new sandbox executions and live paper quality remain **UNVERIFIED**. The ordinary `./sigma test` suite passed all its steps; that validates engineering checks, not usefulness or delight. See [METHOD](METHOD.md), [evidence index](evidence/README.md), [test log](evidence/sigma-test.log) and [coverage ledger](evidence/browser-inventory.json).

## Strongest experiences: small, checkable intellectual turns

**The subset-sum counterexample is the best stock research moment.** A short guessed recurrence survives two transitions and fails at the next. The saved artifact gives actual values and the review distinguishes refuting that recurrence from refuting the project. A researcher can understand the result, challenge it and use it to rule out a direction. This is meaningful negative knowledge, even though the narrative is a fixture. Later history explicitly avoids reviving the false rule.

**The historical Maximum Regions proof repair is the strongest substantive assistance examined.** The first review demands an actual attainment argument. The follow-up computes intersections in an explicit family, and the final Skeptic combines it with the earlier convex-region argument. Reading the record changes “I know the formula” into “I see why the upper bound and construction match.” The saved reviewer also stops rather than demand a redundant cycle to repeat the final sentence. It is bounded, comprehensible and independently checkable. It is standard mathematics, not novel discovery, and no formal checker ran.

**Some starting briefs are excellent.** Jesus/Cannabis separates regional evidence from personal attribution and repeated retellings from corroboration. The zeta ladder separates convergence, continuation, identities and RH. Xenopus and catalysis insist on novelty boundaries, independence and confounding rather than calling a literature summary a discovery. These are strengths of the prepared questions and human strategies. Their presence does not establish that the product's agents follow them.

**The ordinary manual workspace works.** Saving, eligibility toggling and reopening were reliable throughout. Domain-specific continuations could be written without a provider connection. Text exports preserved the question and plans. The copper visual system is coherent and the narrow layouts fit. These qualities lower the cost of keeping a research notebook. They become distinctive only if the notebook also helps decide what the evidence means.

## Weakest and misleading experiences

The most consequential reproduced defect is **confidence inflation in the presentation layer**. Nonconsecutive Session 1 is saved as `incomplete` and Metrics says Incomplete, but the Solution review says “Convincing but unverified.” The UI maps every known non-verified state to that label. A user reading the closest detail view can receive a stronger confidence signal than the stored record warrants. This is not merely awkward wording. [Artifact screenshot](evidence/nonconsecutive-artifact.png), [expanded record](evidence/nonconsecutive-subset-sums-expanded.txt).

The historical Maximum Regions rendering has the reverse problem. It says substantive argument was not captured while displaying a complete argument in the Skeptic response. The historical export lacks newer structured argument fields, so conservative projection obscures usable reasoning. “Historical unstructured argument available” would preserve uncertainty without implying absence. In the same view, achieved findings coexist with stale “still open” tasks. [Current rendering](evidence/maximum-regions-archive-initial.txt), [screenshot](evidence/maximum-regions-archive-conclusion.png).

The subset fixture demonstrates another warning: **correct output can hide wrong reasoning**. Session 3's Gaussian-binomial formula is right, its exponent explanation is wrong, and its scripted Skeptic accepts it. Session 5 repairs the explanation. Independent evaluator checks confirm the finite counts and the corrected arithmetic. This is not evidence about the frequency of live-model errors; it is direct evidence that the UI must help inspect the decisive step rather than promote reviewer agreement.

Empty samples expose a different weakness. Binary counting and handshakes ask for short explanations but open into research orchestration and paper controls. Warp Physics invites large speculation before instantiating a toy model. Narrow Passage supplies a long scenario before a first bounded decision. These are not observed failed investigations. They are weak starts where the user must manufacture the first tractable question. The same neutral workspace can be coherent across domains while still being insufficiently helpful in each.

## Patterns across domains

**Mathematics needs a local proof state.** The strongest examples distinguish conjecture, finite check, derivation and verification. Exact constants, boundaries and attainment matter more than the number of cycles. The alternating-zeta and theta briefs focus the right hazards, but have no actual derivations. RH's routes are still research programs until a concrete lemma/operator is instantiated. The elementary samples need a compact explanatory path rather than the same apparatus used for RH.

**Science needs evidence readiness and identifiability before synthesis.** Catalysis first needs to establish whether comparable pathway pairs and leakage-resistant groups exist. Xenopus first needs stage/time/replicate/batch and phenotype coverage. The oscillation sample explicitly has no observations, so a discriminating experimental design could be a successful result. None of these samples contains imported evidence. Evaluator source attempts were mixed: the cited Xenopus article was accessible; GEO presented a challenge and the chemistry publisher pages were inaccessible through the retrieval service. That does not prove SIGMA source failure or invalid citations. It underlines an unverified dependency. See [source checks](evidence/SOURCE_CHECKS.md).

**Engineering needs a chosen object and auditable quantities.** Warp Engineering appropriately distinguishes geometry from hardware, but no metric, units calculation or feasibility artifact is present. A justified unresolved physical requirement could be valuable progress. Producing a staged program alone would not demonstrate it.

**Scenario work needs premise-sensitive comparison.** Narrow Passage's fictional boundary is explicit. Useful assistance would identify which recommendation changes under altered assumptions, with uncertainty and decision dependencies intact. No saved scenario analysis exists. A polished plan would not by itself validate forecasts or actor cooperation.

**Creative work needs creative objects.** Arrival's brief carefully separates story engines, prescience rules and continuities. The last strategy nevertheless bundles treatment writing, integration and multiple audits. I narrowed the next experiment to a costly scene. The product must eventually show whether criticism improves a character decision or story mechanism, not just produces a completed checklist. No scene was generated here.

**Historical inquiry needs source distance.** Jesus/Cannabis has strong scaffolding but no actual testimony, passage or archaeological source. A well-supported “not established” could be excellent progress. There is no basis to score an absent historical conclusion.

## Root causes beyond isolated point fixes

Three layers are not yet reconciled: immutable source records, session-level judgments and the researcher's current working understanding. The artifacts retain old claims and obligations, which is right. The current UI and fork draft often repeat them without showing what was superseded, resolved or carried forward. Fixing one stale label will not solve the broader state problem.

Evidence identity is preserved better than evidence navigation. “Artifact 1” next to an Outcome is plain text, while prior-session source buttons do navigate and expand the relevant session. The rich record is nested under several sections and often repeats itself. A UUID is useful audit data but not an explanation or a usable citation by itself.

The common interaction vocabulary centers execution: Play, cycles, calls, sessions and authoring bounds. These controls matter, but the researcher's questions are “what changed?”, “why should I believe it?”, “which assumption matters?” and “what should I try next?” The app needs a coherent answer to those questions across a whole project. Passing each control's test does not create that experience.

Finally, prepared sample ambition exceeds demonstrated workflow readiness. Excellent prompts can create an illusion that the environment is equipped to perform source searches, data audits or independently validated science. The RH sample explicitly states tool limits, which helps. Other tasks should expose their current capability/input boundary equally clearly, before execution or paper authoring.

## Agent orchestration versus human research progress

An ended session with completed calls establishes that execution stopped. A successful sandbox run establishes that code returned output. A positive agent review establishes what that agent said. A saved paper establishes a communication artifact. None alone establishes that a researcher learned something defensible.

Research progress becomes visible when a failed claim has a decisive counterexample, a missing proof step is supplied, a competing explanation is ruled out, or a negative data audit changes the next decision. The two richer histories contain examples of the first two. The rest of the inventory has not demonstrated those transitions. The offline checks can all pass while the reader still has to reconstruct the answer.

SIGMA's potentially distinctive combination today is **multiple approaches, preserved criticism, computations with source/output provenance and human-editable continuation**. In this study it is most valuable as an auditable argument notebook. The distinctive *live research partner* claim remains unverified. The next improvement should make that existing evidence easier to think with before adding more autonomous activity.

## Top five improvement priorities

1. **Make epistemic state faithful and consistent.** Preserve incomplete, conjectured, refuted, convincing-but-unverified and independently verified distinctions across headline, detail, Markdown and export. Represent historical unstructured evidence explicitly. Evidence: the subset confidence-label defect and the historical proof downgrade. Benefit: the user can trust what a status means before investing in detailed reading. This is the highest priority because inaccurate confidence corrupts all later decisions.

2. **Maintain a current claim/obligation ledger with explicit history.** A claim should link to its evidence, objections, disposition and superseding result. A fork should start with the reconciled current state and a proposed next question, with old failures expandable. Evidence: stale Maximum Regions attainment duties and duplicate/missing next questions in subset forks. Benefit: resuming research becomes choosing the next uncertainty rather than reconstructing the past.

3. **Put the answer and decisive evidence one interaction apart.** Lead with the best-supported local result, what failed, what remains and why. Make each evidence label navigate to the exact argument, code/output or source passage; retain the full audit record below. Evidence: plain-text artifact labels, working prior-source controls and a 26,723-character HTML report for a compact geometric proof. Benefit: users can challenge claims quickly and share a useful research document instead of an organized transcript.

4. **Require a bounded first experiment and visible readiness gate.** Before a session, identify the concrete object, assumptions, available tools/data, local success/failure criterion and resource ceiling. Suggest a scope appropriate to difficulty; let the user edit it. Evidence: empty scheduler/science/warp pools, RH's uninstantiated programs, and manual reduction of the binary-counting limits. Benefit: failed or budget-limited runs can still produce actionable knowledge, and missing inputs are discovered before long execution.

5. **Adapt comparison and communication to the domain.** Support proof/constant comparisons for mathematics, data/replicate tables for science, units and requirement traces for engineering, premise-sensitive choices for scenarios, and competing scenes/decisions for creative work. Let reports be concise research narratives with audit appendices. Paper setup should show a task-specific plan and evidence readiness, rather than leading with global ceilings such as 593 provider calls. That is a ceiling, not observed usage. Benefit: the interface starts helping with the intellectually interesting work rather than merely accepting domain text.

These are product directions, not implementation authorizations or seventeen tickets. Their ordering follows evidence and researcher benefit, not estimated engineering effort. Live user trials and model evaluation would still be required after any changes.

## Proposed end-to-end acceptance standard

Engineering checks remain necessary. Add a small set of **held-out, domain-appropriate research journeys** whose success is evaluated by a person independent of the run:

- Starting from Home, the researcher can state the project goal, current evidence boundary and chosen bounded test without reading a long transcript. Observe time and errors; a proposed initial target is two minutes, to be validated in user trials rather than declared passed here.
- The strategy creates a concrete test/object and includes a failure condition. The user can narrow it, exclude other strategies and understand the actual proposed call/cost ceiling before launch. Missing tools/data produce a useful planning or blocked state, not invented evidence.
- A skeptical turn must materially test a decisive step. Include a planted but plausible derivation error, an attractive unsupported scientific causal claim and a changed scenario premise. Agreement without checking does not pass.
- After completion or stopping, the researcher can identify one defensible change in knowledge, what did not succeed and the best next discriminator. A useful negative result can pass; a polished research program with no assessed claim cannot.
- Every important claim is one direct navigation away from its supporting reasoning/source/computation and the strongest unresolved objection. Confidence labels remain consistent in UI and export. Test complementary bounds/constructions without inventing a contradiction between them.
- Reopen on a later day and branch: the current obligations and supersessions are visible; resolved objections do not silently become new mandatory work. The user can redirect and then verify whether the agent used that instruction. No silent history rewriting.
- A reader who did not see the session can understand the exported result, caveats and unsuccessful approaches. A paper must communicate the supported research state, not upgrade an idea or fixture into a discovery.
- Record actual elapsed time, provider calls, token metadata where supplied, unknowns and grounded cost separately from perceived value. Judge whether the result justified them. Finish with the researcher's independent “would I use this again?” and the reason.

Run both deterministic adverse cases and separately authorized, budgeted live trials. Passing fixtures must remain distinct from live quality. Include at least a short proof, a negative/partial result, a data-limited scientific design, a source-critical investigation and a creative/scenario comparison. Product acceptance requires convincing journeys in these categories, not a numerical average or a total of green checks.
