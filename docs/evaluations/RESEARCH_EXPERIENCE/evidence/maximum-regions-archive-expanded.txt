Method: Criterion: the kth inserted line adds at most k regions. To prove attainment, it suffices to show that each proposed L_k meets its k−1 predecessors at distinct points. The following exact argument establishes both statements; no numerical experiment is needed. 1. Intersection calculation and distinctness. For 1 ≤ i < k, a point on both L_i and L_k satisfies ix + i² = kx + k². Thus (i−k)x = (k−i)(k+i), and division by i−k ≠ 0 gives x = −(i+k). Substituting into either equation gives y = −ik. Therefore L_i ∩ L_k = { (−(i+k), −ik) }. For fixed k, distinct indices i give distinct x-coordinates, so these k−1 intersection points on L_k are all distinct.
