Prime Factorization and the Euler Product v1

For complex s with Re(s) > 1, investigate the relationship between the Dirichlet series ζ(s) = Σ(n≥1) n^(-s) and products indexed by primes. Determine and prove any exact factorization, with precise convergence conditions. Separate finite-product identities from infinite limits and justify every interchange using absolute convergence or another explicit bound. Check a small numerical case with a certified tail estimate. Explain what prime-power information follows from logarithmic differentiation, and why that does not by itself prove the Riemann Hypothesis. Distinguish recalled standard results from facts derived in this investigation; do not invent sources or claim computation that was not performed.
