Theta Symmetry and the Zeta Functional Equation v1

Assume the Gaussian theta transformation θ(t) = t^(-1/2)θ(1/t) for t > 0, where θ(t) = Σ(n∈ℤ) exp(-πn²t). Starting from the Dirichlet series definition of ζ(s) on Re(s) > 1, derive a meromorphic continuation and the functional equation for the completed zeta function, with all normalization constants stated and checked. Then derive the resulting symmetries of nontrivial zeros and explain why symmetry about the critical line does not imply that every zero lies on it. Justify the Mellin transform, integral split, analytic continuation, and every interchange of sum and integral. Treat the theta identity as a supplied premise, not as evidence that this investigation proved Poisson summation. No outside references or computation tools are available.
