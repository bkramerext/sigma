Counting Pairwise Handshakes v1
===============================

Project
-------
Interpretation: Agent-generated findings are provisional unless explicitly verified; reviewer agreement is not proof.

Research problem: A group has n people, and each pair shakes hands exactly once. Determine the total number of handshakes for general positive integer n. Justify the counting argument and state clearly whether a person may shake their own hand.

Strategies
----------
Direct counting (saved)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Derive a general count from the stated setup. Check small group sizes, explain why every handshake is counted exactly once, and identify any assumption that changes the answer.

Check unordered pairs without self-pairs (saved)
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Label people 1..n and count pairs i<j. Check n=1,2,3 and compare summing each person's partners with the double-count correction. Stop once the bijection and assumptions are clear. Evaluator-authored plan; not an AI result.
