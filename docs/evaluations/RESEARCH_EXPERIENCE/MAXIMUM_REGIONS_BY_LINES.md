`maximum-regions-by-lines` — For each integer n >= 0, determine the maximum number of regions into which n distinct straight lines can divide the Euclidean plane. Lines may be placed arbitrarily. Derive a formula for the maximum, prove that no arrangement can exceed it, and show that an arrangement attaining it exists for every n. Include the n = 0 case and check small values. Do not assume a particular proof method.
