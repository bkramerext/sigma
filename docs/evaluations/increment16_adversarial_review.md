# Increment 16 adversarial research-quality review

## Verdict

The new mechanism checks independently reproduce two counterexamples, assert domain-specific obligations, and now **fail** on keyword-only or numerically tampered purported follow-ups. This strengthens archival integrity and prevents a weak evaluator from crediting a research transition solely for topical language. It does not constitute semantic proof, a blinded expert quality judgment, an unscripted model run, or a comparison against an equally funded single-role researcher.

## Specific evidence and limits

- **Graph insertion:** For edges 0→2 and 1→0, proposed insertion 2→1 closes the directed cycle 2→1→0→2, despite the absence of 1→2. The archived follow-up asks for a reachability-based replacement and comparison with recomputation. The independent checker reconstructs this directed path from edges instead of trusting the agent response. A general dynamic-DAG algorithm, its complexity and large-scale correctness are unproved.
- **Subset recurrence:** For candidate a(n+6)=4a(n+3)+a(n), n=0 yields predicted 9 but independently saved counts give a(6)=8. Subsequent research asks about phase-resolved residuals, explicitly retaining the missing global recurrence/limit proof. This is a useful rejection of a false candidate, not a newly discovered general theorem.
- **Evidence ON/OFF:** The same bounded question generated different *scripted* next questions; both request n=0,1,2 boundary checks. There is no independent basis for preferring the phase-product question to the six-state transition baseline on research quality. The causal result is on orchestration, not scientific merit.
- **Skeptic:** T17.8 contains a genuine real-model repair to an incomplete proof, but no matched self-review control. It cannot settle the multi-agent architecture choice.

## Adversarial falsifiers

The audit fails when a downstream prompt lacks the full recorded witness, silently flips the graph acyclicity judgment, changes the numerical counterexample, or merely includes words like 'counterexample', 'reachability', and 'residual'. This intentionally covers **structural evidence consistency only**. A malicious or mistaken prompt could repeat an exact witness and still draw a false logical conclusion; no semantic claim of correctness is made.

## Engineering versus research decision

Keep the witness-grounding check because it is cheap and catches real provenance errors. Do **not** add expensive language-matching metrics or claim we have measured the value of multiple agents. Increment 16 can be accepted as a bounded adversarial causal-mechanism audit after exact-head CI passes. Its central research-quality hypothesis, superiority over a strong self-review baseline, remains **unanswered**. Advance to 17 only with explicitly framed archival continuity evidence rather than invented replication.
