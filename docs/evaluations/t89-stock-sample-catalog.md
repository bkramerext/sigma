This is scripted fixture evidence, not live model work. The historical `maximum-regions-by-lines` provider export inspected by PR #74 is supplementary evaluator material, not part of that sample's shipped saved history. No provider calls, generated paper, or real research outcome were made for this audit.

`domain` and `difficulty` below are the effective UI/catalog values. Legacy `research_mode` values decode into domain; omitted difficulty remains `None`. “No saved outcome” means there is no stock session outcome to assess, not that a research attempt failed. Educational purpose describes the learning opportunity intended by the prompt, not a result achieved by SIGMA.

## Findings and owner decisions

1. **No retirement is recommended from this evidence.** For 15 samples, the study observed prepared briefs and manual persistence but no SIGMA research outcome. Their research quality is unobserved, which is not evidence that they have no educational value.
2. **Consider a warp-drive pathway decision.** Engineering and Physics overlap, yet their different boundaries may support a staged learning path. Decide whether to retain independently or consolidate; no source is removed here.
3. **Consider first-step scaffolding for broad/empty samples.** The strongest candidates from PR #74 are RH, the scheduler, science/catalysis, Warp Physics, Arrival, and Narrow Passage. Any substantive prompt/strategy changes need owner approval.
4. **Review the PR #74 trust defects separately.** It reports an `incomplete` outcome rendered as “Convincing but unverified” for a deterministic subset-sum session, and historical unstructured argument obscured in the Maximum Regions archive. This catalog PR does not change UI projection or research history.
5. **Review missing difficulty values.** `counting-constrained-binary-strings` clearly fits Easy and is corrected here. Difficulty for `jesus-cannabis`, `nonconsecutive-subset-sums`, `operation-narrow-passage`, and both warp-drive projects requires product judgment; none is silently guessed.
6. **Keep RH as flagship.** No change to its title, research problem, strategies, or history is proposed.
