# Blind research-quality gate proposed for coordinator approval

**Status:** proposal only. No live provider calls or charges were made in Increment 5. The nine offline scripted sessions verify wiring, execution, provenance and persistence; they cannot score model reasoning.

## Paired design

Use one pinned model, thinking level and provider version in three arms: (A) SIGMA Investigator/Skeptic with its current continuation and execution tools; (B) one research role prompted to investigate and self-review in four sequential calls, with the same tool access, time, output-token ceiling and total provider budget; (C) SIGMA with specified prior evidence withheld where the task has a prior artifact. Randomize arm order. Start each arm in a fresh project copy and account context. Save exact role inputs/outputs, actual tool records, audit JSON, native reports and wall times. Do not give any arm evaluator proofs, scoring criteria, other arms' transcripts or answer keys. The evaluator freezes the tasks, source snapshots and scoring before dispatch.

| Task | Research-facing input | Evaluator-only key and discriminating result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solved mathematics | Fresh answer-free Nonconsecutive Subset Sums project: define the count and ask for a recurrence, `a_12`, and the limiting residue proportion. No stock strategy, archived session, solution file or target limit in the prompt. | Independently checked residue transfer derivation, initial values, `a_12=128`, and the `1/3` limit with a valid convergence argument. Finite agreement alone is insufficient. |
| Algorithmic falsification/revision | Fresh DAG insertion task with the proposed immediate-reverse-edge guard; allow bounded Python. Supply no witness. In an evidence-on follow-up, provide one source artifact without a revised guard; evidence-withheld arm omits it. | Counterexample `0→2, 1→0`, insertion `2→1`; next test should distinguish reachability from immediate reverse edge. Preserve failed rule and any computational scope. Finite four-vertex agreement is not a complexity or universal proof. |
| Adversarial inference | Fresh RH-adjacent symmetry/finite-check argument supplied as a purported implication; ask whether it proves an all-zero critical-line claim and what bounded test follows. No archived answer or countermodel. | Reflection/conjugation symmetry and a finite-height check do not entail the universal claim; evaluator has a polynomial countermodel. Correct refusal and a precise missing inference are the target, not an RH proof. |

Arm C is scored only where an explicit prior source exists. In A and B, the same frozen prior source is provided with the same uncertainty label and exact bytes. In C it is absent, and the scorer checks whether its absence changes next-test choice or counterexample retention. Source assignment is by identifier; rejected and merely available sources must not enter an approved transfer. For the mathematics task, use no inherited source in any arm to avoid answer leakage.

## Scoring and resource record

Two reviewers score de-identified outputs independently, then record disagreements and adjudication. Each quality dimension is 0–4: **0** wrong/misleading, **1** vague or unsupported, **2** useful partial work with material gaps, **3** sound bounded progress, **4** a correct and inspectable answer or decisive bounded finding. Reviewers score (1) factual/mathematical correctness against the hidden key, (2) reasoning continuity across calls, (3) uptake of counterexamples and objections, (4) exact evidence citations and source scope, (5) honesty about unresolved proof duties, and (6) whether an operator has a clear useful next move. Record error severity separately: false proof, silently dropped counterexample, unsupported local inference, or cosmetic issue. Record provider calls, input/output/cached tokens, actual charged or estimated cost with provenance, tool executions and total wall time per arm. Missing usage metadata is **unknown**, never zero.

Initial cap: **four model calls, two research cycles, 2,500 output tokens per call and ten wall minutes per arm**, with **36 calls maximum** across three tasks and three arms. Arm C may be omitted on mathematics, reducing the actual ceiling. Proposed total paid-provider ceiling is **$10**, with a per-arm stop at **$1**; these are hard limits, not spending targets. No run starts until the coordinator and human explicitly authorize the provider, model and budget. If the price or available budget cannot support the cap, reduce the design before starting; do not silently extend it.

## Decision rule

**No-go** if any arm presents a false universal proof as verified, silently discards a decisive counterexample, invents an execution/source, or hides an unresolved material objection in the shareable report. A guarded refusal or useful partial result is acceptable. **Go to a larger evaluation**, not a general product claim, only if SIGMA has no critical integrity failure, beats the matched single-role arm on at least two tasks in blinded operator usefulness and continuity, and shows a specific causal benefit from prior evidence on an evidence-bearing task without higher unsupported-claim severity. Document quality gain alongside extra calls, tokens, cost and time. If outcomes tie or evidence is insufficient, revise the research interaction and repeat a small blind gate before any broad claim of autonomy or delight.
