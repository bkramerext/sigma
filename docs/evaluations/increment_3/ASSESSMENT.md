## What is mathematically supported

Independent exact subset enumeration gives `a_5=5`, `a_4+a_2=4`, so the short recurrence fails at `n=2`; `a_12=128`. The complete subset-sum generating polynomials match the `q^(k²)` Gaussian-binomial formula through `n=18`. The gap-removal shift `k(k−1)/2` plus the ordinary subset shift `k(k+1)/2` is `k²`. These finite checks do not prove the order-12 global recurrence or the `1/3` limit.

The existing rational polynomial oracle checks reflection/conjugation symmetry, six exact roots and off-line high roots despite all low-height roots lying on the line. It refutes the RH-SYM/RH-FIN **logical inference from those premises alone**. The polynomial is not zeta and supplies no counterexample to RH. See [oracle output](independent-oracles.json) and the evaluator-only [oracle script](../../../browser_checks/continuation_oracles.py).

Neither fixture agreement nor an uptake label certifies these facts. A role must identify whether a source is a negative constraint, unverified input or method to test. A saved local counterexample cannot be deferred, rejected or used with an affirmative treatment. This is a structural/provenance guard, not a semantic proof checker: dishonest free text can still make an invalid inference, and unscripted agent judgment remains unverified.

## Product judgment

**Would I continue? Maybe.** I can now deliberately carry a known failure and a complementary method into a bounded fork, inspect the original evidence, and see that the next session received it. That is a useful researcher action. It reduces the chance of starting from a blank slate or repeatedly proposing the same disproved candidate.

**Meaningful progress: partial, at the level of a research plan and durable evidence transfer.** The control generated no new derivation, experiment or novel theorem. Independent arithmetic confirms the inputs; it does not validate the scripted planning preference. **Trust: conditional.** Source lineage and exact dispatch are inspectable. Semantic relevance, correctness, Skeptic independence and usefulness of an unscripted next step still need a separately authorized provider evaluation.

The experience is not yet delightful. Full-page captures expose extremely long reading surfaces: Current Understanding, inherited claims, Conclusion, detailed Outcome and activity repeat material. Evidence ON's Conclusion exceeds eight thousand characters in this small control. The inherited-source panel gives usable links and honest scope, but a researcher still has to work through dense prior prose. The obligation list also contains many separately phrased duties; there is no demonstrated semantic reconciliation or agenda ranking. This increment does not repair those larger product problems.

Concrete remaining opportunities:

- **High: relevance and uncertainty.** This control proves transfer mechanics, not that a real model rejects a subtle invalid handoff or selects the best test. Next acceptance should measure independently scored, unscripted choices under an explicit human budget.
- **High: readable continuity.** After a few branches, opening saved research yields repeated claims and very long scrolling. Present a short current question, the exact decision that changed, a few necessary sources and unresolved duties, with full history available on demand.
- **Medium: approval/export lifecycle.** Approval companions are stored with the working project and copied into sessions/audits. Editing an approved approach requires approval of a fresh fork. Conversion of an approval-bearing audit into a stock sample is explicitly refused rather than silently dropping lineage; portable round-trip sample conversion is not implemented here.
- **Medium: source selection scale.** Approval currently exposes saved findings without semantic relevance ranking. The researcher chooses a bounded packet (eight accepted sources, 64 KB); overflow is refused without truncating evidence. Projects with many related failed candidates will need better supported selection, not a natural-language equality shortcut.
- **Medium: failed-run interpretation.** The stop/restart control ends with its proof duties open and too little budget for another pair. The engine preserves that fact, but the user must still distinguish budget exhaustion from an intellectual stopping point.
