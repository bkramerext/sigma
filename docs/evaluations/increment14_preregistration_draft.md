# Increment 14 preregistration history

This initial draft is superseded by [T114 revision 2](T114_MATCHED_BUDGET_PREREGISTRATION.md), which is the only active protocol proposal. The earlier draft's one-repeat 12-pair treatment, role-specific objection-uptake scoring, and `+0.40` decision threshold were withdrawn after adversarial review. Do not use this historical note to freeze or execute a study.

The current proposal has three repeats per task/evidence variant (36 pairs), treats the six tasks as the independent clusters, normalizes both arms to the same six-field output, removes role-specific scoring, and requires external curator, key-custodian, grader, isolation, price and explicit authorization gates. It remains proposed, not frozen or authorized.
