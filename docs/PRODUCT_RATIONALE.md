# Product Rationale

AI assistants can produce compelling answers quickly. But a convincing answer is not necessarily a reliable research result. Difficult questions often require a succession of partial insights, failed approaches, competing explanations, and careful scrutiny of evidence. Without a durable record, an investigation can lose the distinction between what was proposed, what was tested, and what remains uncertain.

**SIGMA exists to make research cumulative.** A question can be explored through different strategies, revisited across sessions, and informed by earlier findings, failures, objections, and open questions. Progress does not always mean solving the entire problem. Ruling out a promising but mistaken direction, clarifying a disputed assumption, or identifying the next decisive test can also be worthwhile outcomes.

**Persistent Research Knowledge** is a logical view of the saved project record, not a separate database or a collection of established truths. Findings, failed approaches, hypotheses, objections and uncertainties remain distinguishable. Today, later turns can use bounded selections of earlier local and peer evidence, and oversized dispatch context can be compacted without changing saved originals. Improving context relevance, coverage of unresolved questions and economy is architectural direction, not a claim of exhaustive retrieval. Selected strategies and bounded sessions remain under human direction; the optional Supervisor guides the process rather than the conclusions. Current Understanding and optional papers are interpretations of that record, subject to human review.

It also aims to make **disagreement productive**. An Investigator develops ideas and a Skeptic independently examines the reasoning and evidence, contributes alternatives, redirects inquiry or identifies missing information. The value is not that two agents produce more discussion. Criticism should change what happens next: strengthen an argument, improve an experiment, narrow a claim, or expose a dead end.

SIGMA uses distinct Investigator and Skeptic roles to challenge proposed reasoning, identify weaknesses, and preserve unresolved objections. Currently, both roles use the same underlying language model. This provides structured adversarial scrutiny, but it is not independent validation by a different model; both roles may share systematic biases or blind spots. Using different models for investigation and review is a potential future enhancement to evaluate, not a capability committed to a particular release.

**Evidence must be distinguishable from assertion.** A model's report that a computation ran is not the same as a saved execution record; a successful computation does not by itself establish a general claim; and agreement between agents is not independent verification. SIGMA aims to keep the reasoning and its limitations inspectable, without treating a mathematical proof, an experimental result, and a historical inference as interchangeable forms of evidence.

The same principles should be useful in mathematics, science, historical research, and complex scenario analysis, even though those fields demand different kinds of justification. SIGMA is intended to support that breadth without claiming any single procedure can certify every conclusion.

The central proposition must also withstand scrutiny: **does persistent, multi-agent investigation produce better-supported, more useful research than a simpler AI assistant operating with comparable resources?** That advantage has not been established. Testing it is part of SIGMA's purpose. And we're prepared to simplify SIGMA if the answer is no.
