Replace nested three-group menu with a flat accessible menu, ordered by lifecycle/importance. Include inactive Schedule, Fork, Report artifact with explanations, then separated destructive Remove. Change strategy link label to `Go to strategy: <strategy title>` with dynamic title; keep underlined text styling and ID-based expand/scroll/focus navigation.

## Outcome: intellectual result, not execution status

13. Above Model/Progress/other session metadata show a concise, prominent **Outcome:** with a color-coded assessment and substantive caveats. This is the main user-facing summary; it must be grounded in saved role outputs, skeptical critique and Coordinator decisions, not a UI guess.
    - **Green / Positive:** meaningful substantiated progress or a useful established result.
    - **Copper / Mixed or inconclusive:** partial progress, unresolved issues, promising leads with material caveats.
    - **Red / Negative:** the approach is invalidated or no useful path remains; mention any useful negative findings or residual leads.
    Use text labels/icons as well as color, for accessibility. Reserve copper as the normal outcome accent, with green/red where assessment warrants. Avoid exaggerated claims.
14. **Operational state and intellectual outcome are independent.** `Failed` session status is reserved for execution/provider/agent-system failure, not for a mathematical dead end. A completed session may have a negative or mixed outcome. A failed session can preserve valuable partial insights, clearly marked incomplete. Pending/running sessions should show a provisional assessment only if evidence supports it; otherwise `Outcome: Pending`, not an invented conclusion.
15. The outcome should say what was achieved, what went wrong or remains uncertain, and whether any promising possibility survives, proportionate to evidence. Model agreement is not independent verification; a claimed proof of RH must not be called a verified solution. Extraordinary results need explicit verification caveats and no celebratory certainty based solely on agent claims. Preserve machine-readable assessment and summary in durable session records or derive deterministically from durable evidence; exports and reopen must agree.

## Coordination and export audit

16. Audit the observed session that stopped after **one Investigator/Skeptic cycle (2 of 6 calls)** because the Skeptic recommended stopping. Inspect actual outputs and Coordinator branch before tuning. Skeptic criticism alone is not an automatic stop; Coordinator must distinguish a fatal disproof, useful obstacle worth revising, productive conclusion, and resource exhaustion. Use available remaining rounds only when there is a concrete next test/question; do **not** force all rounds. Make targeted, testable prompt/decision adjustments if evidence warrants. Record baseline and post-change decisions with offline mocked cases including recoverable objection, decisive counterexample, no progress, and a positive result. No speculative new orchestration framework.
17. **Project export must permit reconstruction** of each session: strategy/problem/model/billing/limits snapshots; ordered Investigator, Skeptic and Coordinator attempts with timestamps, statuses and resource use; validated public outputs, critiques, decisions, artifact IDs and links, unresolved obligations, terminal reasons, errors and interrupted steps, and Outcome assessment with caveats. Audit current export before changing it; close concrete gaps only. Never export credentials, private reasoning, or provider raw envelopes. Verify round-trip consistency and preservation of previous projects.
18. Improve failure diagnosis and usability where directly related: distinguish operational errors from negative research outcomes; show clear completion reason and keep artifact inspection primary. Do not turn this into A18's deeper mathematics or a global dashboard.
