Finding and change

Cloud identity rendering left the local project header without the required
Google sign-in pill. The cloud project header also displaced the existing
ChatGPT connection control. The project header now shows a visibly unavailable,
noninteractive Google Sign-in / Coming soon status for local workspaces, while
cloud workspaces retain their Google identity controls and the ChatGPT control
remains present. The narrow layout preserves Model | Google above Thinking |
ChatGPT and keeps Difficulty separate. Existing Model, Thinking, Difficulty,
and ChatGPT handlers were not rewritten.
