Review concern 1 — newest prior-session failures:
The failure collector now sorts all failed/interrupted attempts by their attempt timestamp, newest first, across sessions. It uses finished_at, then started_at, then session timestamps as a fallback. The regression deliberately makes session creation order disagree with attempt recency and verifies the newer attempt is supplied first.

Review concern 2 — deferred handoff storage bound:
A strategy may now retain at most 24 deferred peer handoffs, in addition to the four-item active prompt batch. Once that cap is reached, SIGMA retains an overflow count and at most eight compact recent omission records (with source/target artifact IDs and handoff IDs); older omission records are counted. Coordinator output exposes the overflow summary. This bounds per-branch deferred storage while making overflow visible.
