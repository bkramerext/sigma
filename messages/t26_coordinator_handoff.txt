Remaining limitations / blockers
No implementation blocker remains. Sage is optional and large, pinned to amd64;
arm64 testing used emulation with 20–30s cold startup and up to ~48s for helper
interfaces. Stop may wait for bounded completion/cleanup, not immediate kill.
Admission remains per backend process, with one active research session; there
is no host-wide multi-process scheduler. Local image availability is an
observation, not a guarantee of later startup success. Legacy pre-portfolio
sessions cannot dispatch computation and receive truthful unavailable guidance.
Agents receive plot/file metadata, not full binary contents (T28 remains).
Real-model tool-selection/reasoning quality and formal proof are unverified.
Docker daemon/kernel/image remain trusted. Runtime code has no network.
