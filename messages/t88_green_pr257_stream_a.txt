The mock research fixture is deliberately narrow: it advances one enabled,
saved strategy through queued, simulated Investigator/Skeptic, and result
states. With simulated allowance available and provider state Connected, the
completed fixture may store one explicitly labeled, deliberately inconclusive
artifact; exhausted sponsored allowance or simulated provider unavailability
ends in failure without an artifact. Mock allowance amounts are displayable
fixture state, not real balance, reservation, or consumption. The mock provider
selector does not connect to or verify a provider. Server middleware continues
to deny the normal live research-start endpoint, `/api/ai/*`,
`/api/cloud-research/*`, and project writes outside the explicit safe-write and
mock-route allowlist, preserving the no-provider-charge boundary.
