During the 0.1.1 backup gate, a consistent snapshot
sigma-state-issue265-20261027-075405 reached READY. Its clone was mounted
read-only on a temporary recovery VM without a public IP or service account.
The packaged restore verifier failed because its fixture placed
workspaces.sqlite3 and cloud-research.sqlite3 at /auth, while the actual
production layout stores both under /auth/workspaces. The verified snapshot
and live disk agree on that layout. This PR corrects those paths, treats the
two database files as permitted workspace-root metadata, and updates the
fixture to represent production.

With the corrected verifier copied only to the recovery VM, the read-only
restore check passed: all three SQLite databases integrity and tables, two
saved identities and workspace mappings, three project records, ten research
artifacts, zero paper pairs, and accounting references including one
reserved/unknown call. Temporary VM and clone disk were removed; snapshot
is retained. No production release change, provider call, or secret exposure.

Tests: tests/state_backup.py passed (10 tests); `./sigma test` passed,
including Ruff, Pyright, frontend typecheck and production build.
The production UI remains on the old release pending SMTP credential and
deployment of reviewed 3f2426c6fa83866c655cb1b601a7e2df78e5e459.
