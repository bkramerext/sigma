## Research judgment and remaining limits

**Would I continue using SIGMA here? Maybe.** I would use this integrated build for bounded computation and evidence review: it now connects a saved question, actual execution, observed objections, next dispatch, concise Outcome and full audit. I would not treat the scripted next-test choices, hash-bound interpretations or optimistic agent agreement as independently valid research. A blind unscripted comparison with a matched simpler assistant is still needed to show that SIGMA improves reasoning enough to justify its time, cost and complexity.
