## Trace and root cause

The pill's local reconnect path can call `ai.signIn()` without `sponsor: true`
and include its selected ChatGPT account ID. That posts to
`/api/ai/auth/begin`. The local API guard recognized the configured sponsor
ChatGPT email and unconditionally returned “Use Admin Settings to renew the
sponsor account,” even when the request carried a verified Google admin
principal. The Google admin email and ChatGPT sponsor email are distinct
identities; the route must authorize by Google role and choose the sponsor
credential from the protected sponsor store.

`auth_begin` now routes a selected sponsor credential through sponsor OAuth
when the verified local Google principal has the admin role. Other identities
remain forbidden. The sponsor-store read transaction is closed before OAuth
begins; the initial regression run caught that nested store acquisition would
otherwise wait indefinitely.
