Reproduced baseline weaknesses before refactoring:
- 110 long artifacts assembled to 308,902 bytes and blocked dispatch even
  though optional history crowded out a recent disproof.
- The 12-entry prior-session limit silently omitted an early concrete disproof
  from 13 findings and provided no omission count or source pointers.
- A 70,000-emoji question passed a character-count check at 70,394 characters
  despite occupying 280,394 UTF-8 bytes, above the 256,000-byte limit.
- Saved steps had no canonical selection manifest or human-readable reason for
  selected/omitted sources.
These are deterministic observations, not claims about how a live model reasons.

Implemented:
- Deterministic, bounded contexts for legacy and multi-strategy agents: all
  unresolved obligation sources, current Investigator output for Skeptic review,
  newest artifact and marked counterexamples/retractions precede optional
  history. Deterministic extracts retain links to originals; source history is
  not rewritten. Historical obligations are not resolved by omission.
- Skeptic obligation dispositions are restricted to supplied evidence and exact
  basis artifact/step IDs, and remain labeled model-reported/unverified.
- Prior-session disproof selection, newest-first metadata-only failures, peer
  source/domain/assumption/computation/retraction context, and active guidance
  have bounded omission counts and source pointers.
- UTF-8 input and manifest storage bounds fail closed before provider preflight
  when mandatory content cannot fit. The branch pauses with an audit event and
  preserves all saved artifacts; no provider request or hidden charge occurs.
- Each dispatched step persists its context manifest before provider dispatch.
  The existing Provenance disclosure and text/HTML/LaTeX reports expose source
  categories, reasons, representation, byte budget, and omission details.
- Current main integration exposed a 320px overflow in project-header controls.
  A narrow-screen grid track fix preserves the controls and prevents horizontal
  overflow.

Known limitations:
- Deterministic tests show what evidence is selected and preserved; they do not
  establish that a model notices or reasons correctly about it. No live model
  quality evaluation was authorized.
- Model-reported addressed/superseded/retracted obligation dispositions are
  epistemically unverified and should not be treated as proof.
- Context selection is deliberately bounded (24 current artifacts, 12 peer
  artifacts, 64 obligations, 256 KB UTF-8); mandatory overflow pauses for human
  narrowing rather than attempting AI summarization.
- T17.8's independent research-quality gate remains separate and unchanged.
