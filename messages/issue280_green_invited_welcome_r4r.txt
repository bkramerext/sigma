## Review scope

This branch implements the sponsored allowance and local force-mode behavior,
invitation/reload dialogs, welcome message, administration table changes,
session progress text and toast cap. The sponsored pill displays the balance
from the signed-in account's local ledger, with a custom hover/focus/mobile
explanation. Server checks enforce eligibility, provider choice, and spending
limits. Hosted `SIGMA_CLOUD_FORCE_SPONSORED_MODE` remains default-off.

Local sponsored inference uses the designated `zetacontinuation@gmail.com`
ChatGPT sponsor by default, even when `SIGMA_CLOUD_INFERENCE_PROVIDER` is
unset. The signed-in member's ChatGPT session is not used. Connection checks,
provider credential validation and ledger job records share this selection.
Local spending is reserved against the member's configured grant. `--sponsored`
does not enable `--mock-auth`; mock identities and the simulated `$25`
allowance still require `--mock-auth` explicitly. At zero allowance, the pill
offers **Reload me!**.

The local Play expiry report exposed a saved `personal` mode for the eligible
Google account `nontrivialzeero@gmail.com`, while its pill still displayed
“Sponsored ChatGPT.” The designated sponsor record existed but had no usable
access or refresh token. The current fix returns an expired personal choice to
sponsored mode, makes the pill follow the selected mode, and uses the designated
sponsor account even when another ChatGPT account is active. A sponsored pill
click starts personal ChatGPT sign-in and selects personal mode only on success.
The local administrator can renew the existing sponsor credential from **Admin
Settings → Reconnect sponsor ChatGPT**. Member Google sign-in cannot renew it.
Live Play remains blocked until that administrator sign-in completes.

Follow-up after the reported reconnect guidance error: a metadata-only read
found two designated sponsor registrations, both without access or refresh
tokens. Rerunning the server alone cannot renew them. The shared local store
also allowed an active sponsor registration to appear in personal mode or be
revoked by the ordinary disconnect endpoint after an admin renewal. The server
now reserves that registration for sponsored inference, blocks ordinary
renew/disconnect operations on it, and selects a usable duplicate even when
the shared billing mode is API-key. This prevents member personal inference
from using the sponsor credential outside the sponsored spend ledger. The
administrator must still complete the interactive ChatGPT renewal.

The welcome appears on each fresh page entry for an eligible signed-in user,
but not on browser refresh. It starts with “Welcome to SIGMA.” and one of 100
distinct curiosity-themed lines. Invitation and reload requests remain
administrator-reviewed. Other changes include Privacy Notice return paths,
SIGMA action-button styling, invitation Add/Remove actions and row filtering,
compact scrollable authorized-user rows, five visible toasts with oldest-first
eviction, and the 50-word running-session summary with fallback text.

The switchable local sponsored pill's custom tooltip now starts “Click to
switch to your own ChatGPT account.” It retains the remaining allowance and
connection explanation. Forced/hosted pills do not show a switch instruction.
Real-Chrome sponsored acceptance and `./sigma test` passed after this wording
change. A metadata-only recheck still found no designated sponsor tokens.

The member-facing failure no longer asks the member to contact or act as an
administrator. It reports temporary sponsored-service unavailability and
preserved research. Token-free, rate-limited `sponsor_connection status=...`
diagnostics and `sponsor_oauth status=...` events appear in backend logs for operator
diagnosis. The designated sponsor still needs a valid server-side credential;
the change does not use the member's ChatGPT account or an API-key fallback.
Focused cloud-auth/sponsor tests, real-Chrome sponsored acceptance, and
`./sigma test` passed on this correction. Live Play remains unverified until a
designated sponsor credential exists on the server.

Local diagnostics now also append fixed, token-free event codes to
`sponsor-diagnostics.log` in the checkout root. The 0600 file is Git-ignored,
64 KiB bounded, and rejects unsafe replacements. This lets a developer in the
same checkout inspect the failure without asking a member to find terminal
output. Hosted service logs remain unchanged.
The developer read the resulting local file after an offline sponsor-status
check: `sponsor_connection status=credential_missing`, two designated records,
no token refresh/model call. The file was 0600 and 71 bytes. Focused tests,
real-Chrome sponsored acceptance, and `./sigma test` passed.

Configuration and rollback are documented in `docs/GETTING_STARTED.md` and
`docs/CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md`. Rollback disables the hosted flag and restarts using
the guarded release procedure. Production was not enabled.
