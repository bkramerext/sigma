## Changes

Newly invited eligible accounts now get a centered “You have been invited!”
dialog after sign-in, including reloads before acknowledgment. The allowance
copy uses the effective allowance for the signed-in account when it is $1.00 or
less. A new account gets “We will start you off with a $X.XX allowance.”;
accounts with usage or holds get “We added a $X.XX allowance to your account.”
One of 100 distinct curiosity/research-themed lines is selected per dialog.
Escape and the acknowledgment button dismiss it, and dismissal is persisted
server-side exactly once. A database migration gives existing accounts a
default of no pending welcome. Browser state does not carry allowance across
account switches.

The existing force-sponsored-mode flag configuration and rollback procedure
are documented in `docs/CLOUD_DEPLOYMENT.md` (section “Hosted sponsored-mode
selection”): the flag defaults off, only applies alongside sponsored access,
and rollback sets it to false followed by the guarded release/start procedure.
Production configuration was not changed and the flag was not enabled.
