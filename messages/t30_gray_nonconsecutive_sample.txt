Sample changes

The existing `samples/nonconsecutive-subset-sums` sample now carries a sanitized
`sample-evidence.json` sidecar with five saved sessions. Its `project.json`, math
problem, and three strategies are unchanged. The history contains:

1. Negative: a deterministic exact enumeration refutes the tentative
   a_(n+3)=a_(n+2)+a_n recurrence at n=2 (a_5=5, while a_4+a_2=4).
2. Mixed: roots-of-unity recurrence candidate and a_12=128, supported by finite
   exact DP, with matrix and asymptotic obligations open.
3. Mixed/productive: gap-removal and the q-binomial polynomial formula, with
   finite exact polynomial checks and the global recurrence/limit unresolved.
4. Mixed: a later transfer-matrix pass consults earlier findings, avoids the
   refuted short recurrence, and checks a candidate order-12 step-three relation
   by exact DP through n=60; it does not claim a global proof.
5. Mixed/productive: a follow-up corrects an exponent explanation in session 3.
   Under the standard one-based Gaussian-binomial convention the subset baseline
   is k(k+1)/2; together with the gap shift k(k-1)/2 this yields k^2. Exact
   polynomial comparisons through n=18 support the formula while its all-n proof
   remains open.

All Investigator/Skeptic responses came from deterministic local fixtures and
are explicitly marked as not live-model output in the saved outcomes and
artifacts. The Python programs were actually run in the isolated local research
sandbox and their code, output, status, and runtime metadata are preserved.
No live or paid provider calls were made. The first attempt at session 4 was
rejected by SIGMA because the fixture incorrectly supplied a formal peer
citation absent from that single-strategy call; the fixture was corrected to
refer to prior-session notes as notes, and the successful session remains
inconclusive. No failed attempt was copied into the sample.

The new browser check is documented in `browser_checks/README.md` and verifies
all five saved outcomes, their collapsible detail areas, session metrics and
provenance, the corrected q-binomial artifact, the exported report, and no
horizontal overflow at 390px/320px. The report is about 35.3K characters.

Load and review instructions

1. Start the local application with `./sigma start`.
2. On Home, open Samples, expand Mathematics, and choose
   “Nonconsecutive Subset Sums Modulo 3”. This creates a working copy; the
   catalog template stays unchanged.
3. Open Sessions #1–#5 and inspect each Outcome, especially Metrics, Provenance,
   and Citations, Evidence & Obligations. Session #1 is the counterexample;
   Session #5 shows the q-binomial convention correction.
4. To read the full deterministic history outside the UI, use the project
   toolbar's Download action and choose a Text file or HTML report.
